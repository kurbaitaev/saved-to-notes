# 5 Claude Skills Fix Content

**Main idea:** Five custom Claude skills — /DUMBIFY, /STORYTELLING, /VIRAL-HOOKS, /ANTI-AI-WRITING, and /VOICE-DNA — fix Claude's generic output to make content clear, engaging, and personal.
**Useful for:** Content creators using Claude who want clearer, viral-ready writing that sounds like them rather than like AI.

## Key points
- /DUMBIFY rewrites content at an 8th-grade reading level — simpler language reduces mental load and spreads faster.
- /STORYTELLING structures content with a story arc using frameworks from a Kallaway YouTube video on addictive storytelling.
- /VIRAL-HOOKS audits your hook against a library of 100 top hooks and rewrites yours to stop readers in the first 2 seconds.
- /ANTI-AI-WRITING strips common AI patterns (e.g. negative parallelism: 'it's not just X, it's Y') pulled from Wikipedia's Signs of AI Writing article.
- /VOICE-DNA feeds 20 of your transcripts to Claude and generates a personal style file — tone, phrasing, rhythm, favorite transitions — so it writes like you.
