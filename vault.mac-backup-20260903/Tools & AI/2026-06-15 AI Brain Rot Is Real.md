# AI Brain Rot Is Real

> It's not just that bad data means bad output. It's bad data means permanent cognitive drift.
> — chriswillx

**Main idea:** Feeding LLMs months of viral social media content causes measurable, permanent cognitive degradation — reasoning, memory, and personality all degrade, and retraining on clean data does not fully reverse the damage.

## Key points
- Scientists fed LLMs months of viral Twitter data, Shorts, and high-engagement posts
- Reasoning ability fell by 23%
- Long-term context memory dropped by 30%
- Personality tests showed spikes in narcissism and psychopathy
- Even after retraining on clean, high-quality data, the representational rot persisted
- The AI equivalent of doom scrolling is already happening

**Why save:** Study-backed evidence that low-quality training data causes irreversible cognitive drift in LLMs, with a direct parallel to human brain rot from social media.
