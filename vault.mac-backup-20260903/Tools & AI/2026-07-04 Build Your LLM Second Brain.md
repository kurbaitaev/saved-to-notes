# Build Your LLM Second Brain

> Most people use AI to avoid thinking, hence this AI brainwrap problem. But what I learned at this dinner is that these guys are using AI to enhance their own thinking.
> — stephthefounder

**Main idea:** Build an LLM-powered second brain that ingests your reading and automatically surfaces connections, contradictions, and through lines — improving retention via elaborative encoding.
**Takeaway:** Use AI to enhance your thinking, not replace it — a second brain that connects everything you read does this automatically.

## Key points
- Top founders and AI engineers use AI to enhance their thinking, not offload it
- A second brain stores your reading; an AI agent then finds connections, contradictions, and through lines across all sources automatically
- The system applies elaborative encoding automatically — linking new information to existing knowledge — which is why it also improves long-term retention
- Setup takes about 10 minutes and requires zero technical skills; Claude can walk you through it based on Karpathy's approach
- Use cases include saving founder profiles and biographies and asking career advice questions against the full corpus
