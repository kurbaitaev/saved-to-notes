# DIY Photo Zine One Sheet

**Useful for:** Anyone who wants to turn personal photos into a printed photo booklet without design skills, Photoshop, or glue.

## Steps
1. Open dirtylittlezine.com in your browser
2. Upload your photos to the panels (vacation shots, portraits — anything)
3. Add captions to panels if you want
4. Click Print your Zin — a PDF downloads automatically
5. Print the PDF (one sheet of paper)
6. Fold along the printed fold lines
7. Make one cut in the middle
8. Done — a finished mini photo magazine
