# Reel Quality in One Minute

**Useful for:** Creators who want to quickly improve the visual quality of their Reels using CapCut.

## Steps
1. Open CapCut and apply the shown color settings to make the image more vibrant
2. Add filters (any of the suggested ones) and set their intensity to ~30%
3. Use two font pairs only: one for the title, one for the body text — don't mix more
