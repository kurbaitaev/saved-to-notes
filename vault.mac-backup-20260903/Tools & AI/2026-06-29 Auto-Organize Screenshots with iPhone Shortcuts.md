# Auto-Organize Screenshots with iPhone Shortcuts

> Your iPhone already knows what's in your screenshots. It might as well do something about that.

**Useful for:** Anyone who accumulates screenshots and wants them automatically sorted into named albums each night without manual effort.

## Steps
1. Create a Shortcut that finds every screenshot taken today
2. Use Repeat with Each to loop through them one at a time
3. For each screenshot, use Apple Intelligence to pick the best album from your list (e.g. travel, tasks, shopping, other)
4. Save the screenshot to the chosen album and move to the next
5. Go to Automation and schedule the Shortcut to run every night
