# Claude + Metricool: Social Media Autopilot

> Самое крутое, что он сам планирует и публикует посты в лучшее время.
> — Кирилл Ликс ❘ Нейросети и Маркетинг

**Useful for:** Content creators who want Claude to read their social media analytics, analyze competitors, generate content ideas, and auto-schedule posts.

## Steps
1. Search 'Metricool MCP' → scroll down to the setup page → copy the MCP URL: https://ai.metricool.com/mcp
2. Open Claude.ai → Customize → Connectors → '+' → Add custom connector
3. Name the connector 'Metricool' and paste the URL
4. Create a Metricool account and connect your social networks (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Threads, YouTube, etc.)
5. Prompt Claude: 'Use Metricool and look at top posts from competitors and give me 10 content ideas based on what works'
6. Claude can also auto-schedule and publish posts at the optimal time based on analytics
