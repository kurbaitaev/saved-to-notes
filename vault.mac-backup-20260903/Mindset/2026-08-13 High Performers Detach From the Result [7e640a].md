# High Performers Detach From the Result

> Let go of the result. Just take the shot.
> — Ridhi Gupta

**Main idea:** High performers don't procrastinate because they've detached from the outcome — procrastination is an attachment problem, not a discipline problem.

## Key points
- High performers 'don't give a shit about the output' — they focus on the process, not the result.
- Procrastination is an attachment problem, not a discipline problem.
- Detaching from the result removes the fear that stops people before they even start.
- High performers aren't fearless — they've just stopped letting the output define who they are.

## Transcript

The biggest difference between high performers and procrastinators isn't talent or motivation, it's just that high performers don't give a shit about their foot. Think of it as a basketball player, every unseen practice, every missed shot that no one claps for. He's not thinking about this scoreboard, he's just shooting, and since he's not attached to whether it goes in or not, he can focus on the next shot. Psychologists call this flow state detachment. When you detach yourself from the result, you remove the fear that stops people before they even start. The same fear that convinces you it's a disciplined problem when actually it's an attachment problem. High performers aren't fearless, they've just stopped letting the output define the kind of person they are. Let go of the result, just take the shot.
