# Original Thoughts Are a Muscle

> You're not trying to be someone else. You're just trying to find out who you are underneath all this content consumption and borrowed opinions.

**Main idea:** Independent thinking is a learnable muscle, not an innate trait — practice it by sitting with your raw reactions before the internet answers for you.

## Key points
- People with interesting original ideas simply sit with ideas long enough to develop something to add — they are not born that way.
- The internet's lack of friction (read → share instantly, no processing) is what makes everyone converge on the same opinions.
- Capture your raw reaction to a movie, book, or idea before you check reviews, YouTube, or the comment section.
- Half-formed thoughts are still thoughts in progress — don't suppress or discard them.
- When you disagree with something, first state that you disagree, then figure out why — the figuring-out is where independent thinking is built.
