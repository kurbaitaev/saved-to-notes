# Prioritize Fun to Unlock Creativity

> When you're having fun, you are so much more creative, you make better decisions, you don't burn out as fast.

**Main thought:** Prioritizing fun over hustle culture leads to more creativity, better decisions, and sustainable business growth.
**Takeaway:** Identify one thing that genuinely feels fun and bring it back into your work today.

## Key points
- Niche into work that feels like play — build a career around what you genuinely enjoy
- Chase curiosities even when they're not perfectly strategic
- Schedule fun activities to create productivity anchors and something to look forward to
- Allow a whatever-I-feel-like budget in your content or projects alongside the strategic work
- Drop rigid routines and work based on energy without shaming yourself for it
- Treat projects as low-pressure experiments — a flop is a learning, not a failure
