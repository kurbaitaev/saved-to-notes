# Travel Before the Winning Streak

> Give yourself permission to go travel. And just know that that is a fundamental strategy in becoming successful.
> — charlie

**Main idea:** Travel right before a major breakthrough is a repeating pattern across Steve Jobs, the Beatles, and Phil Knight, and is supported by a research study of 26,000 artists and scientists.

## Key points
- A claimed study of 26,000 artists and scientists found travel was the common factor before their greatest ideas (specific study not independently verified — closest found is a Nobel laureate mobility study from Ohio State / NYU Abu Dhabi)
- Steve Jobs went to India in 1974 before co-founding Apple
- The Beatles went to Hamburg, Germany, where intensive club performances shaped their sound before global fame
- Phil Knight traveled the world and landed in Japan, where he discovered the shoe manufacturer that directly led to Nike
- Creator attributes his own winning streak to a year of travel after a 2017 brain tumor: he later helped produce what he calls the biggest Nike commercial ever, won an Emmy, reached 9M social media followers, and built a large nonprofit

**Why save:** Packages a memorable research claim and three iconic founder examples into a single principle — travel as a deliberate pre-breakthrough strategy — making it easy to recall and repeat.
