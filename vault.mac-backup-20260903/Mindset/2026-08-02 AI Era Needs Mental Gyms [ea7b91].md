# AI Era Needs Mental Gyms

> We only created gyms because the Industrial Revolution made it possible to live a life without physical exertion.
> — bitterbuilds

**Context:** The creator uses the gym-as-Industrial-Revolution-response analogy to argue that AI will do the same to cognitive effort, making reading and writing the new essential discipline.
**Main thought:** Just as the Industrial Revolution made physical effort optional and gyms compensated, the AI revolution is making mental effort optional — so deliberate reading, writing, and AI fluency will become the equivalent compensation.
**Takeaway:** Reading and writing are the mental gym of the AI era.

## Key points
- The Industrial Revolution created the need for gyms by removing physical exertion from daily life.
- AI is doing the same for cognitive and mental exertion.
- Reading and writing will grow in importance as deliberate cognitive exercise.
- AI fluency is an additional skill that will matter alongside reading and writing.

**Why save:** A compact, memorable analogy for why deliberate cognitive habits will become more valuable — not less — as AI automates thinking.

## Transcript

I read a really interesting quote the other day that said, we only created gyms because the Industrial Revolution made it possible to live a life without physical exertion. And now, because the AI revolution is making it possible to live a life without cognitive or mental exertion, we need the mental equivalent of gyms. This is exactly why I think reading and writing are going to become so important in the coming decades. This is, of course, in addition to AI fluency.
