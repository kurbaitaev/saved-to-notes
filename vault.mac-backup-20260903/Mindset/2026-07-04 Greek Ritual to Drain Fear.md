# Greek Ritual to Drain Fear

> What remains after fear is my true form.
> — gali_kash

**Main idea:** Ancient Greek athletes used a two-step pre-competition ritual — vividly imagining defeat, then affirming their true form — to strip fear of its power.
**Takeaway:** You can't be scared of what you've already accepted — pre-living the loss disarms the fear.

## Key points
- Fear is unprocessed imagination; accepting the worst outcome drains its power.
- Visualize losing in full, vivid detail the night before a challenge — not to discourage yourself, but to desensitize to the fear.
- After visualizing defeat, repeat 'What remains after fear is my true form' until it feels true in your body.
- This reveals your true form after fear, judgment, and expectation fall away.
