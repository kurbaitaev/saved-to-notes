# Easy and Hard Don't Exist

> You don't have easy and you don't have hard. You have familiar and you have unfamiliar.
> — thomasisntreal

**Main thought:** Easy and hard are illusions — what we call talent or natural ability is just accumulated familiarity from repeated exposure to similar things.
**Takeaway:** Stop calling things hard; call them unfamiliar, then go do them more until they aren't.

## Key points
- You're not naturally good at things — you've just done things similar to that before, or your body has a physical advantage that still requires practice on top
- What separates people is who keeps going after failing, not who finds it easy
- Skill is purely a product of repetition — you become good at something by doing it more

**Why save:** Reframes difficulty from fixed ability to learned familiarity, making any new skill feel approachable rather than gatekept by talent.
