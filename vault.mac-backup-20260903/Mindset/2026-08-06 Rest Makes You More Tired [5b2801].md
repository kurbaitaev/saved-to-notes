# Rest Makes You More Tired

> the more you rest the more tired you become
> — tayneboniface

**Main thought:** Inertia applies to human productivity — extended rest kills momentum and makes restarting far harder than staying in motion.
**Takeaway:** The best way to recharge is to keep moving and maintain momentum, not to fully stop.

## Key points
- Extended rest makes even simple tasks feel impossible — not out of laziness but loss of momentum
- Actively pursuing a goal builds momentum that reduces the effort needed to keep going
- Breaking momentum requires far more energy to rebuild than it takes to maintain
