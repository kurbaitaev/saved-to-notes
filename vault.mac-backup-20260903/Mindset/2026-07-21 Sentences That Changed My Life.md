# Sentences That Changed My Life

> the degree to which a person can grow is directly proportional to the amount of truth he can accept about himself without running away
> — emoryy.m

**Main thought:** Seven psychological and philosophical statements about self-awareness, purpose, healing, and potential — presented as sentences the creator read that changed their life.
**Takeaway:** All defeat is psychological, and growth begins only when you can face the truth about yourself without flinching.

## Key points
- Until death, all defeat is psychological.
- One day you'll realize no one was really watching and you could have done what you wanted.
- Your lack of personal purpose is causing your romantic obsessions.
- You must develop a strong opinion of yourself so you don't internalize the beliefs others have of you.
- The degree to which a person can grow is directly proportional to the amount of truth he can accept about himself without running away.
- Sometimes you don't need time to heal, but need more experiences to show your nervous system a different reality.
- You will always want what is in your field of potential — so if you want it, it means you can have it.

**Why save:** A dense, quotable collection of psychologically sharp insights that reframe defeat, purpose, healing, and desire in non-obvious ways.
