# Fall Short of a Big Goal

> I would rather fall short of a big goal and consider myself a failure than lower the goals to the level which I can accomplish all of them just so I can feel like a success.
> — Simon Sinek

**Context:** Sinek argues that aiming high and falling short is more honorable than shrinking your goals just to guarantee a feeling of success.

**Why save:** A sharp reframe on failure — Sinek positions lowering your goals to avoid failure as a worse outcome than genuinely falling short of an ambitious one.

## Caption

Simon Sinek (born October 9, 1973, in Wimbledon, London, England) is a British-American author and inspirational speaker whose work focuses on leadership and organizational culture. His revolutionary concept of "Start With Why" has redefined how individuals and organizations approach purpose, creating environments where trust and inspiration can flourish.

His philosophy in this video challenges the conventional fear of failure: greatness is found in the pursuit of ambitious targets, not in the safety of easy victories. He believes that true success lies in the courage to aim high and fall short, emphasizing that a growth mindset is forged by the willingness to accept failure as a necessary byproduct of visionary thinking.
