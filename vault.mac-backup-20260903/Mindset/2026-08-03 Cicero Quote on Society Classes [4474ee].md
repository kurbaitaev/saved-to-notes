# Cicero Quote on Society Classes

> The poor man works nonstop. The rich man lives off the poor man's labor. The soldier protects them both. The taxpayer pays the bill for all three. The bank robs all four. The lawyer stands in court for all five. The doctor sends a bill to all six. And the criminal terrifies all seven. The politician, he lives like a king off all six.
> — nedwagner

**Context:** A quote attributed to Roman orator Cicero in 43 BC, describing how each societal class exploits or burdens the next — framed as proof that human power dynamics have never changed.
