# Climbing for the View or Visibility

> Are you climbing a mountain for the world to see you or for you to see the world?
> — lulu.broski

**Main thought:** A single rhetorical question challenging whether your ambitions are driven by the desire for external recognition or genuine personal experience.
**Takeaway:** Do things for the view, not to be seen.

**Why save:** A concise, memorable question that cuts to the core of intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation.
