# Do It in Seasons

> i want to do everything so i do nothing
> — storieswithkiki

**Context:** Why ambitious people with many dreams get paralyzed instead of moving toward any of them — and how to reframe it.
**Main thought:** Dream paralysis isn't laziness — it's the weight of too many meaningful dreams making the cost of choosing the wrong one feel unbearable.
**Takeaway:** Do it in seasons: pick one major focus and one minor focus per chapter of life, and start with the dream that scares you most.

## Key points
- It's not laziness or lack of ambition — it's the opposite: the dreams are so heavy and so many that the weight of choosing pins you in place
- You don't have to do it all at once — do it in seasons
- Each season: pick a major focus and a minor focus, and let it develop before the next idea pulls you away
- The fear isn't about choosing wrong — it's about not trusting yourself enough to choose at all
- The only way to build self-trust is to start
- Date the dream that scares you the most right now and see where it takes you
