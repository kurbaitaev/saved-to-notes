# Dirty Fuel vs Clean Fuel

> I am already enough. So let's go use that enoughness to do something incredible with the limited time that I have been given.
> — diewithoutregretss

**Context:** A coaching framework for overachievers distinguishing fear-based motivation (dirty fuel) from wholeness-based motivation (clean fuel).
**Main thought:** Most overachievers are driven by a subconscious fear that ties their self-worth to achievement, and the antidote is shifting from that fear (dirty fuel) to a place of already feeling enough (clean fuel).
**Takeaway:** You can't think your way into clean fuel — the subconscious beliefs driving dirty fuel were formed emotionally and must be rewired the same way.

## Key points
- Dirty fuel = the chip on your shoulder, the 'Fuck you, I'll prove you wrong' energy — powerful but fear-based
- The underlying belief of dirty fuel: 'If I don't achieve X, I am not worthy of Y' — where X is money/status/success and Y is love/respect/belonging/peace
- When self-worth is tied to achievement: setbacks feel devastating, rest feels lazy, switching off feels uncomfortable, nothing ever feels enough
- You're not just trying to build something — you're trying to outrun something
- Dirty fuel beliefs were formed emotionally through childhood experiences, not logically — they can't be reasoned away
- Clean fuel = 'I am already enough' — movement from wholeness, not lack
- Clean fuel is a cocktail of play, curiosity, service, mastery, and a genuine love of the game
- Shifting to clean fuel requires bringing subconscious beliefs into awareness and doing the work to rewire them
