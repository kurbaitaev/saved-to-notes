# The Real Definition of Failure

> Failing doesn't just mean you got something wrong. Failing means that you got separated and everyone is finding out.
> — beefcakemaxxing

**Main thought:** Your fear of failure isn't about the odds — it's a childhood-conditioned fear of public separation, and the only rational response is volume: you can't predict which attempt wins, so the only lever you control is how many exist.
**Takeaway:** Post about things you're already doing anyway, at volume — you have no crystal ball, so make more.

## Key points
- Angry Birds was Rovio's 52nd game — the first 51 flopped completely (98% fail rate before a global hit)
- James Dyson built 5,127 failed prototypes before the model that still exists today (99.98% fail rate)
- The creator posted 186 times over 6 months to reach 426 followers, then a 30-second Costco grocery run hit 4M views
- None of the three knew in advance which attempt would be the one — volume is the only controllable variable
- Failure fear is socially ingrained: a bad grade in 1st grade meant being publicly moved to a different table, not just getting something wrong — so 'failing' became synonymous with being judged in front of everyone
- Twenty years later, you're not scared of failing — you're scared of being put back in that room

**Why save:** Reframes failure-aversion as a childhood social-conditioning response rather than a rational signal — directly explains why you already know volume works but still don't act.
