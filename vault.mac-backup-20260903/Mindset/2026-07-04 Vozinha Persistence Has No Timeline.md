# Vozinha: Persistence Has No Timeline

> We cheer for persistence differently than we cheer for talent, because persistence looks like a choice, which means it was possible and we could have made it too, and maybe we still can.
> — curiousnoor

**Main thought:** Vozinha's 19-year journey — driving buses, playing across seven divisions — to Cape Verde's historic 0-0 draw against Spain at age 40 is proof that 'not yet' and 'never' are not the same thing.
**Takeaway:** Not yet and never are not the same thing — and most of us make that mistake about ourselves before anyone else gets the chance.

## Key points
- Scouts passed Vozinha at 16 and 18 — always 'not quite enough or not quite ready'
- He drove buses and worked as an electrician to stay close to football while supporting his family
- He played across six countries and seven divisions over 19 years, most matches untracked by stats providers
- Called up for Cape Verde's first-ever World Cup without a club contract, at age 40
- Cape Verde held Spain (ranked #2, reigning European champions) to a 0-0 draw
- His Instagram went from 56,000 to nearly 15 million followers after the match
- The US government expedited his mother's visa after the story went viral so she could watch the next match from the stands
