# Courage Over Intelligence for Action

> I don't think this part of my journey is asking for intelligence or perfection... but I think it is asking for courage

**Context:** A scientist-turned-lawyer-turned-founder reflects on why she posts daily on Instagram despite feeling exposed, framing it as practice for her resilience training business.
**Main thought:** Intelligent people often overthink themselves out of actions that would lead to growth — sometimes courage matters more than intelligence or perfection.
**Takeaway:** Do the awkward, imperfect thing anyway; this stage of the journey asks for courage, not polish.

## Key points
- Smart people frequently rationalize their way out of opportunities or valuable learning experiences
- Posting publicly every day feels vulnerable and weird, but it serves a concrete purpose: practicing speaking and sharing ideas
- Accepting imperfection is not lowering standards — it is recognizing what the current phase actually requires

**Why save:** A concise, lived-experience argument for why action beats overthinking — especially for high-achievers who use intelligence as a shield against discomfort.
