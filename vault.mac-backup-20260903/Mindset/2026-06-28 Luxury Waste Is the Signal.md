# Luxury Waste Is the Signal

> The waste isn't a side effect, it is the message.

**Main idea:** Luxury goods function as costly status signals because the wasteful spending itself — not the object — is what communicates wealth and high status to others.

## Key points
- Signaling requires spending what others cannot afford — the inaccessibility of the purchase is what makes it a signal.
- Once a luxury becomes accessible to many, it loses its signaling power and a pricier substitute is needed.
- Desire that fades when others gain access was never about the object — it was about exclusive status.
- Conspicuous waste works as a signal precisely because it demonstrates surplus resources no one else can burn.
- The pursuit of ever-larger signals can become self-destructive if the fire grows out of control.
- Participating in status-signaling games is a choice — you can decide when to stop.
