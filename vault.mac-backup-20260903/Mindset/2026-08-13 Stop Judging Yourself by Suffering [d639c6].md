# Stop Judging Yourself by Suffering

> nothing grows when we burn away all that is bright and replace it with ash
> — Nuffsaid Channel

**Context:** A spoken-word piece on self-compassion arguing that self-punishment is not a measure of character, and that the impulse to be hard on yourself actually comes from deeply loving life.
**Main thought:** Self-punishment doesn't build character — judging yourself by the length of your suffering is wrong, and you deserve to be happy even when you mess up.
**Takeaway:** You don't have to be perfect to be good.

## Key points
- If being hard on yourself worked, it would have worked by now.
- We judge the depth of our character by the length of our suffering — but we don't have to.
- Hating yourself comes from a deep desire to live life right — that desire is an act of love.
- You are angry because you care so deeply, and you fear wasting this life because you hold it so precious.
- You deserve to mess up and still be happy.
