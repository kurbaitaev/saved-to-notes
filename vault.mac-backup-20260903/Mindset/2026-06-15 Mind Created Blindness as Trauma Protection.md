# Mind Created Blindness as Trauma Protection

> If your mind can create and remove blindness, what else is it creating in your life that you think is permanent?
> — overthinkexpress

**Main idea:** A documented clinical case shows the mind can manufacture and dissolve perceived permanent limitations — blindness in this case was psychological, not physical, created as a trauma coping mechanism.
**Takeaway:** Limits you think are permanent might be psychological protection your mind created that you no longer need.

## Key points
- Patient BT had 10 personalities and had been completely blind for 17 years after a traumatic accident
- Her blindness was cortical — originating in the brain, not the eyes — diagnosed as a result of trauma
- In blind personalities, her brain showed zero visual activity; in a teenage boy personality, brain lit up and she could see normally
- 8 of 10 personalities eventually regained vision during therapy; the remaining 2 stayed blind, holding the trauma
- She could switch between blind and seeing states within seconds

**Why save:** A real clinical case study that concretely illustrates how the mind can create and remove perceived permanent limitations as trauma protection.
