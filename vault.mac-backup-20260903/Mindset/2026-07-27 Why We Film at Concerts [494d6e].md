# Why We Film at Concerts

> Capturing the moment is part of how we have learned to express excitement and manage emotions that can be overwhelming. The capturing of those feelings has become part of how we experience the feelings themselves.
> — miriam_tinny

**Context:** Creator reads two passages from an upcoming book arguing that filming at concerts is an unconscious emotional regulation mechanism, not just a status move.
**Main idea:** We reach for our phones at overwhelming moments not to show off but to regulate emotions we've lost the capacity to simply sit with.

## Key points
- Generating photographic evidence isolates experiences from the torrent of immediacy and makes them feel more real — whether in the capturing or in the sharing.
- Even unposted photos are treated as potential commodities: their imagined social reception determines the photo's value and, in turn, the value of the experience itself.
- We've lost the ability to interpret or sit in feelings of emotional heightenedness — phone use has become a subconscious attempt to process and understand the moment.
