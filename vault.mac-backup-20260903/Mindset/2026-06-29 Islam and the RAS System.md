# Islam and the RAS System

> Verily Allah does not change the condition of people until they change what is in themselves

**Context:** The reel connects the neuroscience of the Reticular Activating System and neuroplasticity with a Quranic verse and Hadith Qudsi, arguing Islam encoded this principle 1400 years before modern science named it.
**Main thought:** Islamic practices like zikr, salah, and gratitude are neurological rewiring sessions that operate through the same mechanisms neuroscience now describes as RAS filtering and neuroplasticity.
**Takeaway:** Your dominant thoughts determine what your brain perceives as possible, and Islamic acts of worship are — neurologically — repeated belief-reinforcement sessions.

## Key points
- The RAS filters perceived reality based on dominant thoughts, emotions, and beliefs — tell your brain what you want and it starts finding it
- Visualization with gratitude and belief physically changes brain structure within weeks: cortisol drops, dopamine rises, better decisions follow
- The brain cannot distinguish between a vividly imagined experience and a real one
- Thinking Allah will provide opens the RAS to see provision; expecting struggle filters out every opportunity
- Zikr, salah, and shukr are neurological rewiring sessions, not just acts of worship
