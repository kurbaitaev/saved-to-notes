# 10 Signs of High Agency

**Main idea:** High-agency people share observable behavioral patterns — from immigrant roots and quitting prestige careers to questioning the question itself.

## Key points
- They or their family are immigrants — brings unusual resourcefulness to get out of tough situations
- They quit something prestigious (investment banking, consulting) to break free of golden handcuffs and embrace uncertainty
- Notorious self-learners who teach themselves without needing institutional permission or validation
- Don't trust what others say — validate everything themselves using first principles thinking
- Grew up with weird, niche teenage hobbies outside the societal norm during peak social pressure years
- Share niche content online without using social metrics as a gauge of intrinsic content quality
- Appearance doesn't match their interests — defying the stereotypes of their own group
- They question the question — asking whether the question being answered is even the right one to ask
