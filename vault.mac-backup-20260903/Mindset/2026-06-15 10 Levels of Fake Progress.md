# 10 Levels of Fake Progress

> Knowing exactly what to do and never doing it. That's the final boss.
> — gxdkov

**Main idea:** Ten behaviors that feel like self-improvement but substitute the feeling of progress for actual action — and the one rule that breaks the cycle.
**Takeaway:** Make proof before any input — finish one hard task before consuming more advice.

## Key points
- 1. Saving the video — you built a graveyard for advice, not knowledge.
- 2. Watching at 2x speed — procrastinating efficiently.
- 3. Taking notes — just handwriting if nothing changes afterward.
- 4. Buying a book — it's the receipt, not the transformation.
- 5. Building the perfect routine — most people just need to obey the one they already have.
- 6. Telling friends your goals — the dopamine hit tricks your brain into thinking you already did it.
- 7. Watching disciplined content while your room is a mess — the tutorial is on the floor.
- 8. Optimizing your setup — your life needs evidence, not another desk light.
- 9. Waiting for a fresh start (Monday, next month, next semester) — being addicted to a new checkpoint.
- 10. Knowing exactly what to do and never doing it — the final boss.

**Why save:** Concise, memorable breakdown of the most common self-improvement traps, with a single actionable rule to escape them.
