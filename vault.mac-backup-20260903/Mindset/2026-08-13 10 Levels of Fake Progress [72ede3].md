# 10 Levels of Fake Progress

> The rule is to make proof before any input.
> — Sasha Gudkov

**Main idea:** Consuming advice, taking notes, buying books, and building routines are all fake progress — they substitute the feeling of doing for actually doing.
**Takeaway:** Make proof before input: finish one real task before consuming any more advice.

## Key points
- 1. Saving the video — you just built a graveyard for advice
- 2. Watching in 2x speed — now you're procrastinating efficiently
- 3. Taking notes — if nothing changes after, that's just handwriting
- 4. Buying a book — the book is not a transformation, it's the receipt
- 5. Building the perfect routine — most guys just need to obey the one they already had
- 6. Telling your friends your goals — that little dopamine hit is your brain pretending you already did it
- 7. Watching discipline content while your room is disgusting — the tutorial's on the floor
- 8. Optimizing your setup — your life needs evidence, not another desk light
- 9. Waiting for a fresh start (Monday, next month, next semester) — addicted to a new checkpoint
- 10. Knowing exactly what to do and never doing it — that's the final boss

**Why save:** A clean, memorable breakdown of every fake-progress trap with a single concrete rule to break the cycle.
