# 10 Habits to Replace Doomscrolling

> Get so used to filling the gap yourself that the scroll stops being the default.
> — xibo_wu

**Main idea:** Replace the scrolling urge with a competing physical or social action that occupies the same hand and same moment.
**Takeaway:** Same hand, different object — the replacement habit has to be as immediate and accessible as the phone.

## Key points
- Do 20 push-ups the moment you feel the urge to scroll — phone stays down until the set is finished.
- Call someone instead of texting — voice only, no typing, same conversation you'd have had in the app.
- Take a walk after dinner — outside, no destination, 15 minutes.
- Clear one physical drawer or folder you've been avoiding — not a digital one.
- Read two pages of a book the moment you reach for your phone — same hand, different object.
- Practice a skill for 10 minutes instead of watching someone else do it — your hands on the instrument, not your eyes on the feed.
- Sit outside for two minutes before going back indoors — nothing in your hands, nothing playing, just sit.

**Why save:** Concrete, immediately executable alternatives for the exact moment the scrolling urge hits, framed as habit replacements rather than willpower.
