## Transcript

Moving to a new country makes you rich faster than anything else. The day you land in a place where nobody knows your name, nobody knows your story, your past, every safety net you've ever depended on, it disappears in one flight. You're sitting in a coffee shop in a city, you're not even fully understanding, trying to figure out how to make a doctor's appointment in a language you're still learning, and that exact discomfort, the loneliness, the awkwardness, that I want to go home but I can't, is the same fire every rich successful person had to walk through to become who they are now. The biggest thing that happens to you is that you finally get to meet yourself. No noise, no expectations, no old roles to play, just hours of silence, where your thoughts have nowhere to hide, you start asking real questions, what do I actually want, who do I actually want to become, what was I tolerating back home that I never even noticed because everyone around me was tolerating the same thing. You read, you write, you build, you pour every hour into you, and by the time you go back to visit, you're not the same person, you don't even fit in the same chair, you don't laugh at the same jokes, you out do the entire town while everybody else stayed exactly where you left them. Comfort never made anything and anybody great, but distance did.
