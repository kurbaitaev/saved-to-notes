# Use the city, then get out

> You have to use the city strategically and intentionally or else it will use you.
> — samouttashape

**Main idea:** Cities are social clubs best used for two purposes — making money and finding a partner — after which you should leave to avoid the trap of working just to afford staying.
**Takeaway:** Have a clear goal for city living or the city will extract value from you indefinitely.

## Key points
- Cities are good for exactly two things: making money and finding a partner — once both are achieved, leave.
- High cost of living = a premium for social club access (bars, restaurants, people) that you pay whether you use it or not.
- The trap: many people work hard to afford the city another month, which lets them work hard to afford the city another month.
