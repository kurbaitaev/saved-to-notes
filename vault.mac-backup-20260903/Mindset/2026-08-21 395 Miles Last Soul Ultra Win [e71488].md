**Main thought:** Mark Dowdle (USA) won the Last Soul Ultra by completing 95 loops — 395 miles (637 km) — over roughly four days, outlasting 159 other starters in a last-person-standing backyard ultra format.
**Takeaway:** Watching someone run 395 miles non-stop reframes what a human limit actually is.
