# You Either Win or Quit

> you either win or you quit. If you try enough times, it eventually will happen.

**Context:** Mathematical principle of ergodic systems applied to persistence — enough repetitions drive the probability of failure to zero.
**Main thought:** In a system where every state has a non-zero transition probability, the system is ergodic, meaning sufficient repetitions make eventual success mathematically guaranteed.
**Takeaway:** Quitting is the only real failure — persistence is mathematically guaranteed to work in an ergodic system.
