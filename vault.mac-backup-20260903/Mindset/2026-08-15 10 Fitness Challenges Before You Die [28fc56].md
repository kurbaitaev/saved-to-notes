# 10 Fitness Challenges Before You Die


## Key points
- Pull-ups / muscle-ups
- Open-water swimming
- Road cycling
- HYROX fitness race
- IRONMAN 70.3 (half-distance triathlon)
- Full IRONMAN triathlon
- 1000 Pound Club — combined 1RM of squat + bench press + deadlift totaling ≥ 1000 lbs
