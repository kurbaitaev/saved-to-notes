# 10 Sports Every Man Should Try

> A dangerous man is comfortable in every environment.
> — herman.1ind


## Key points
- Weightlifting — build the body first
- Martial arts — learn to protect the people you care about
- Swimming — be comfortable in every environment
- Running — mental toughness built one kilometer at a time
- Calisthenics — master your own body weight before anything else
- Cycling — explore the world using your legs as the engine
- Free diving — hold your breath until your mind wants to quit before your body does
- Spear fishing — hunt your own food in the ocean with nothing but a spear
- Rock climbing — problem solving with your life on the line
- Dirt bike riding — controlled aggression at speed

**Why save:** A ranked list of physical disciplines with sharp one-line rationale for each, useful for deciding what to train next.
