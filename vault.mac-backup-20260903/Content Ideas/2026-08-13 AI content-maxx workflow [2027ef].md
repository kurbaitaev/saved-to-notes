# AI content-maxx workflow

> Use AI to speed up your content creation process instead of replacing your creativity altogether.
> — nicktarmo

**Useful for:** Creators and founders who want a repeatable AI-assisted system to ideate, script, and repurpose content across platforms.

## Key points
- Source ideas from long-form content, not short-form — short-form ideas limit you
- Find outlier videos with 3–5× views relative to follower count to reverse-engineer what made them work

## Steps
1. Get content ideas from long-form content, not short-form
2. Pick one high-performing format you can make every day
3. Find a creator who does that format well; scroll their page for outlier videos (3–5× views vs follower count)
4. Copy the outlier video's link and paste it into Stanley; prompt it to transcribe and break down why the video performed
5. Use a prompt to templatize that video's structure
6. Save the high-performing format to Stanley's memory for future reuse
7. Using your idea, format, and analysis, write a rough script in the same chat
8. Ask Stanley to refine it into a final polished, high-performing script
9. Film the video
10. Repurpose the video into a carousel using Stanley's carousel generator
11. Repurpose relevant video scripts into platform-appropriate written content for X and LinkedIn
