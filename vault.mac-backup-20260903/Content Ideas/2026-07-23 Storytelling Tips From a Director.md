# Storytelling Tips From a Director

> we won't remember what you did, but we will remember how a story made us feel
> — victoriamorse

**Main idea:** Great social media storytelling uses a three-part structure — hook, story, takeaway — written like a text to a friend, not a polished script.
**Takeaway:** Write your story like a diary entry or voice note to a friend: focus on how you felt, not just what happened.

## Key points
- Storytelling isn't about being the best writer — it's about making people feel something
- Develop your story before editing; record your voiceover first — it sets the foundation for everything
- Think of your script as a diary entry, not a script
- AI can organize your thoughts but can't tell your story — the why, the feelings, the meaning are yours alone
- Your authenticity is the one thing no one else can copy
- Three-part story structure: Hook (what is my story about?), Story (what am I learning or what's challenging me?), Takeaway (what am I an expert in or how can someone reflect on this?)
- Write like you're texting a friend or leaving a voice note
- Talk about how you're feeling, not just what's happening

**Why save:** Practical, experience-backed framework for authentic short-form video storytelling, distilled from commercial directing work.

## Transcript

I became a better storyteller after directing commercials for a living. One thing I learned is that storytelling isn't about being the best writer, it's about making people feel something. If you watch my editing tutorial, I recommend developing your story before you even start editing. I always record my voiceovers first because it sets the foundation for everything else. Every great movie, TV show, or commercials start with a script. But instead of thinking of it as a script, think of it as a diary entry. Yes, AI can help you organize your thoughts, but it can't tell your story. And maybe Claude can tell people my morning routine, but it can't tell them why that morning mattered what was on my mind or how I've been feeling. And that's the part that people connect with. We're in a time where everything looks and sounds the same, but social media was built for connection, and your authenticity is the one thing no one else can copy. So the next time you sit down to create, start with three parts of your story and ask, what is my story about the hook? What am I learning right now, or what is challenging me? The story? What am I an expert in, or how can someone reflect on this? The takeaway? Then write it like you're texting a friend or leaving a voice no. Because to be honest, we won't remember what you did, but we'll remember how a story made us feel.
