# 4 Ways to Build Viral Sense

**Useful for:** Content creators who want to train an intuitive sense for what makes videos go viral on social media.

## Key points
- The AI viral analysis prompt shown on screen (frame 3) has 5 sections: 1. THE HOOK (psychological trigger, curiosity/identity/pain/contrast/bold claim, why it stops the scroll), 2. THE PROMISE (emotional/practical/identity-based, what viewer expects to get), 3. AUDIENCE TARGETING (who it speaks to, what belief/fear/desire it taps), 4. STRUCTURAL MECHANICS (tension points, pattern interrupts, re-hooks, payoff delays), 5. THE CORE PSYCHOLOGICAL LEVERS (authority signaling, relatability, curiosity gap, FOMO, validation, "you already knew this but never articulated it" effect, contrast/before-after framing).

## Steps
1. Hide engagement metrics while doom scrolling and guess the like and view count of each video to consciously assess its viral quality.
2. On a video with a high ratio of comments, guess what people are commenting about before opening the comments — forces you to identify which element is most interesting to the average viewer.
3. Paste a viral video link into a content AI like Stanley, ask it to transcribe the video and psychologically break down why it performed well using the Viral Content Analyzer prompt (comment "guide" under the original reel to receive the full prompt).
4. On your feed and explore page, actively engage (like, comment, click Interested) only with viral videos and never engage with non-viral ones — trains the algorithm to show you only high-performing content.
