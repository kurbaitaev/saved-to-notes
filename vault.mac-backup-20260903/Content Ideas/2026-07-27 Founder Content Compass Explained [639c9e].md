# Founder Content Compass Explained

> That is what turns an ordinary story into the one people connect with.
> — mehreenisar

**Main idea:** The Founder Content Compass gives founders two tools: four story sources (expertise, beliefs, process, offer) and a three-layer depth drill (what happened → why it matters → why should someone else care) that turns flat posts into emotionally resonant content.

## Key points
- Compass direction 1 — Expertise (North): industry observations, insights, myths, recurring mistakes
- Compass direction 2 — Beliefs (South): opinions, values, lessons that changed how you think
- Compass direction 3 — Process (East): BTS, client transformations, case studies
- Compass direction 4 — Offer (West): products and services you sell
- Story layer 1 — Surface level (Too Basic): state what happened
- Story layer 2 — Why it matters: add the real reason behind the surface fact
- Story layer 3 — Truth (good story): the emotional core — why should someone else care

**Why save:** Compact, repeatable framework for founders who know what to say but can't make audiences feel it.
