# Duolingo Is a Scam Bit

> has anybody taken this scam
> — Francisco Ramos (@framoscomedy)


## Key points
- Comedian calls Duolingo a "scam" as the central premise of the bit
- Mocks absurd Italian phrases Duolingo teaches, e.g. "he drinks the sugar me water"
- Jokes about Duolingo sending messages at 3 in the morning

**Why save:** Stand-up bit on a universally relatable app — good reference for how to build a crowd-work joke around a single absurd premise.
