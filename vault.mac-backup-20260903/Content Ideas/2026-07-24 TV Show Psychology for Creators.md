# TV Show Psychology for Creators

> Nobody in the history of television has ever fallen in love with a one trait character.
> — jun_yuh

**Main idea:** Unforgettable creators run all four human storylines simultaneously — love, identity, inner life, mission — just like every bingeable TV show, instead of repeating one niche.

## Key points
- Every bingeable TV show runs four simultaneous storylines: relationships (who they love), origin story (who they are and where they come from), inner battle (how they hold themselves together or fall apart), and mission (the thing they're chasing)
- Those four storylines map exactly onto the four parts of human experience: who you love, who you are, how you live, and what you offer — jun_yuh calls this the Storytelling Wheel
- Most creators pick only one storyline — their niche — and repeat the same tips, the teaching, the selling; that's a character with one trait and nobody ever falls in love with that
- The Mere Exposure Effect: the more someone sees you across all four dimensions (love, identity, fitness, earnings), the more they trust you and the less they can look away
- Running all four storylines in conjunction means you never run out of content ideas
