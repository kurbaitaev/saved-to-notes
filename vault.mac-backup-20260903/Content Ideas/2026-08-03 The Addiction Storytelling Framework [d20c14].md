# The Addiction Storytelling Framework

> The founders with the best stories win.
> — matthgray

**Useful for:** Founders and content creators who want to use personal storytelling to grow on social media and convert audiences into buyers.

## Steps
1. Line 1 — The Mirror: reflect where the viewer is right now ("You built a business that looks successful, but you're still the bottleneck.")
2. Line 2 — The Friction: name why they're stuck
3. Line 3 — The Realization: deliver the epiphany no one told them ("The problem isn't work ethic. It's that you ARE the system. Replace yourself with systems, everything accelerates.")
4. Line 4 — The Shift: show your own transformation ("I went from 15-hour days to 4-hour days. Burnt out to energized. Trapped to free.")
5. Line 5 — The Invitation: present the CTA as the natural next step ("If this resonates, here's what to do.")

### Slide 4
*Part 1 intro — using a real video example to illustrate the Zeigarnik Effect*

Part 1
Make your stories addictive

My "How to Work Less and Earn More" video hit 598,000 views.

It opened with one line: "If you thought of someone making $730K per month, how long would you guess they're working?"

Then I held the tension and didn't answer it for several minutes. People couldn't click away. It's called the Zeigarnik Effect.

### Slide 5
*Examples of loop-opening phrases and the neurological reason they keep audiences hooked*

Your brain hates unfinished business.

Open a loop and people physically can't leave until it closes.

"What I realized next surprised me"
"This almost killed the business"
"I didn't understand this for years"
"You wouldn't believe what happened next"

These create questions in people's minds.

Their nervous system craves closure and can't stop thinking about it until it's resolved.

### Slide 6
*Part 2 — the four-stage storytelling funnel from scroll-stop to purchase*

Part 2
Use storytelling as your growth engine

People remember stories 22x more than facts
Storytelling is your fastest funnel to revenue

Attention gets people to stop scrolling
attention → trust → belief → action
Trust makes them listen
Belief makes them think, "This could work for me."
Action is when they buy.

### Slide 7
*Facts vs. Stories comparison showing how mirroring the viewer's experience drives trust and action*

Mirror your viewer's reality first.

Show them you've been where they are.

Make them feel understood through your story.

That's how you move them from attention to action and make profits faster.

Facts: "At Founder OS we teach systems that help remove founders from operations"
Stories: "I was working 15-hour days at 22, miserable and trapped in my business. Then I discovered how to work only 4 hours a day, earning $730k/month."

Show people where you were vs. where you are. Tell that story. The founders with the best stories win.

### Slide 8
*Part 3 — SKIMS as a case study: personal story creates a competitive moat products alone cannot*

Part 3
Use Your Story as Your Unfair Advantage

Look at Kim Kardashian's SKIMS vs every other shapewear brand.

Same product. Same factories. But SKIMS crushes them.

Why? People buy into Kim's story - her journey.

Her obsession with shapewear because of the insane outfits she wears.

That's the moat generic brands can't copy.

*Part 5 — how to attach a CTA that feels like the story's resolution, not a pitch*

Part 5
Turn Stories into Sales

Most people make this mistake: They share a vulnerable story, then suddenly: "Join my newsletter! Book a call!"

That jolts people out of the story.

But a good CTA is inevitable. The natural next step.

The story opens a loop. The lead magnet closes it.
