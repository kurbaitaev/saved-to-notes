# Build Forbes-Like Media for Free

> Don't pay $5,000 a year to just get featured.
> — angus.sewell

**Useful for:** Anyone who wants to build a credible media brand using AI agents instead of paying for paid press placements like Forbes Councils.

## Key points
- Forbes Councils is pay-to-play: Standard $2,500/yr + $500 initiation fee (Council Member badge + publish on Forbes.com); Premium $5,000/yr + $500 (adds 3 ghostwritten articles)
- An AI agent can DM influencers at scale using three outreach styles shown on screen: 'The Casual One' (offer a free feature), 'Short & Sweet' (free promo angle), 'The Fan Angle' (fan-to-creator pitch)
- Use a writing humanizer GitHub repo to strip AI tells from articles generated from an influencer's top post
- Carousel AI prompt (Prompt 03): turn finished article into a 7-slide carousel using a carousel repo, generate on-brand images with Nano Banana Pro, export at 1080x1350
- Instagram allows up to 3 collaborators per post — use 'Invite Collaborators' (not just 'Add Tag') to co-author with featured influencers for shared audience reach

## Steps
1. Spin up a social media blog/media account in any niche
2. Find influencers on the platform and DM them using an AI agent with one of three prompt templates (Casual, Short & Sweet, Fan Angle), offering a free feature
3. Once someone agrees, take their top performing post and run it through an AI writing humanizer repo to produce a humanized article
4. Feed the article into a carousel GitHub repo with Nano Banana Pro to generate a 7-slide carousel at 1080x1350
5. Post the article and carousel; use Instagram's 'Invite Collaborators' option (under Tag People) to add the influencer as co-author — up to 3 collaborators per post
6. Repeat at scale until the account grows large enough to land brand deals or hire a creative director
