# Hooks Without Funnels Don't Convert

> Хорошие хуки без воронок — как красивая витрина без продавца
> — chatplace.io

**Context:** Why a strong hook alone fails to turn views into leads — people enter, watch, and leave without taking action.
**Main idea:** A hook only captures attention; without a funnel system connecting each stage (hook → story → interaction → CTA), views never convert into leads or followers.

## Key points
- Hook grabs attention and pulls the viewer in
- Story develops interest and warms up the audience
- Interactive element (e.g. gamification, quiz, reply trigger) creates engagement
- CTA directs the engaged viewer to take a specific action
- A complete funnel increases reach, engagement, and generates new followers and leads — a hook alone does none of this
