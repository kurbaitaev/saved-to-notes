# 9 Content Concepts That Print Money

> if you know any of these and you do content you are missing out on thousands of dollars a month
> — mila.sokolskiy

**Main idea:** Nine niche content-psychology techniques power high-converting short-form video, and mastering them is the edge for TikTok Shop affiliate monetization.

## Recommended
- [ ] ⚠️ [Pattern Interrupt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methods_of_neuro-linguistic_programming) (No dedicated Wikipedia page exists for 'pattern interrupt'; linked to the NLP methods article that mentions the technique. The concept is widely used in sales and content creation to break a viewer's autopilot state.)
- [ ] ⚠️ [Curiosity Loop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clickbait) (No dedicated Wikipedia page. Frame 1 shows the loop as: 1. Highlight a problem → 2. Explain it can be solved → 3. Buyer seeks diagnosis call to learn the solution. Closest Wikipedia concept is the 'curiosity gap' discussed in the Clickbait article.)
- [ ] ⚠️ Hook Stacking (No canonical page found. Refers to layering multiple attention hooks (visual, verbal, text) simultaneously in the opening seconds of a short-form video.)
- [ ] ✅ [Leading Lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading_lines)
- [ ] ⚠️ [Dead Space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negative_space) (Frame 2 shows it alongside a golden-ratio composition overlay labeled 'The power of Composition.' Dead space in this context means intentional empty area in the frame; closest authoritative article is 'Negative space' on Wikipedia, but the exact term is not used there.)
- [ ] ✅ [Authority Positioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Authority_bias)
- [ ] ✅ [Social Proof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_proof)
- [ ] ⚠️ [Trojan Horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viral_marketing) (No dedicated Wikipedia page for 'Trojan Horse' as a content strategy. Wikipedia's Viral marketing article notes: 'like a Trojan Horse, stories carry messages and ideas along for the ride.' The technique wraps a sales message inside entertainment or value content.)
- [ ] ✅ [Us vs Them Narrative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In-group_and_out-group)

**Why save:** Compact named checklist of nine proven content-psychology techniques for short-form video creators monetizing through affiliate platforms.
