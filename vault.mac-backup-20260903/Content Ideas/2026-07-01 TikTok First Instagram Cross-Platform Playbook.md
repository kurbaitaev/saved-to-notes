# TikTok First Instagram Cross-Platform Playbook

> Look at it like if TikTok is the podcast, Instagram is the promo clip.
> — juliabroome

**Useful for:** Creators who want to grow simultaneously on TikTok and Instagram by using each platform's algorithm strategically.

## Steps
1. Open TikTok, go to the search bar, and type 'Creator Search Insights' — filter for topics labeled 'trending' or with an up arrow, meaning the topic hasn't reached its peak yet
2. Create the video for TikTok first: make it longer, chatty, hot-take style, and less polished — this is the podcast version
3. Repurpose that video for Instagram: trim to under 1 minute 30 seconds, make it more polished and concise — this is the promo clip version
4. Add your other platform handle to your bio on each platform
5. Use your captions and comments to actively bridge followers over to your other platform
6. Post to Stories linking or referencing your other platform to close the loop and drive cross-platform growth
