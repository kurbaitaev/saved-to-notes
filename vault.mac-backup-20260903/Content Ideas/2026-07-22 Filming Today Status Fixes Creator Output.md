# Filming Today Status Fixes Creator Output

> My problem is not that I don't have enough content ideas. My problem is that I actually have too many, and I am not spending enough time thinking critically through my filming production schedule.
> — vanessalau.co

**Main idea:** Switching from a passive content calendar to a 'filming today' status in ClickUp — with production notes, content-type fields, and a single inspiration-links column — collapses planning into an actionable same-day filming plan.
**Takeaway:** Replace 'film next week' with 'filming today' so you decide the exact shoot order, required props, and effort level before you hit record — not during.

## Key points
- The typical creator pipeline (idea → script → film → edit → calendar) feels organized but doesn't create urgency to actually film
- The real bottleneck is rarely too few ideas — it's not planning the filming production schedule carefully enough
- A 'filming today' status auto-filters into a flat spreadsheet view so only today's shoots are visible
- Production notes per clip (needs a drink, drink as prop, co-founder on camera, voiceover only) allow batching similar setups and cut setup-switching
- A content-type field signals effort level (B-roll + text vs. speaking on camera) so you can sequence easy and hard shots strategically across a filming day
- Keeping inspiration links and trending audios in one column eliminates tab-switching mid-shoot
- Planning the most efficient filming order for a full month of content in a single sitting is the lever that makes 30-day content possible in 2 days
