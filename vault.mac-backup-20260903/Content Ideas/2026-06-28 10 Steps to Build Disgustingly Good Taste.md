# 10 Steps to Build Disgustingly Good Taste

**Useful for:** Anyone who wants to develop a strong aesthetic sense and creative taste by studying and remixing the work of other artists.

## Steps
1. Learn where to steal from
2. Check these out (find and collect reference works)
3. Learn to consume these intentionally
4. Learn about the artists behind the work
5. Learn to infer the meaning from their work
6. Learn how to extract the idea
7. Learn to recreate it through your own lens
8. Repeat — it compounds

**Why save:** Concise, repeatable framework for developing taste by studying, deconstructing, and reinterpreting the work of great artists.
