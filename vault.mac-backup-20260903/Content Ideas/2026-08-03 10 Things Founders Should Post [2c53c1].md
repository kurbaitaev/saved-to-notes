# 10 Things Founders Should Post

> People don't follow businesses, they follow founders whose journey they actually care about.
> — noahh.white

**Main idea:** Founders should post about their real thinking, decisions, and behind-the-scenes reality — not just achievements — because audiences follow journeys, not brands.

## Key points
- What you believe your industry gets wrong
- A problem you're currently trying to solve
- A decision you made and why you made it
- A mistake that changed how you operate
- Something a customer said that stuck with you
- The part of the business nobody gets to see
- What you refuse to compromise on
- Your honest opinion of an industry trend
- Proof of how you think, not just what you achieved
- Where you're trying to take the company next

**Why save:** Concrete list of 10 founder content angles that build personal brand by showing thinking and journey rather than outcomes.
