# 5-Part Video Script Framework

> I'm going to walk you through probably the greatest script writing technique.
> — briarcochran

**Useful for:** Video creators who want a repeatable structure for scripting reels and short-form content.

## Steps
1. Hook — pattern interrupt; sole job is to stop the scroll.
2. Super hook — drive the pain point or dream outcome to bridge viewers to the climax; invoke curiosity without resolving it yet.
3. Thesis — one sentence: the belief system or core claim; pop the first layer of curiosity and give the answer to the opening question.
4. Illustration — example, metaphor, story, or statistic to reinforce the thesis; embed a rehook before it ('I'm going to teach you how to do this in a second, but first…') to sustain watch time.
5. Application — two or three steps, a framework, or a one-step takeaway; the practical closing deliverable.

**Why save:** Concise breakdown of a 5-part script structure with the reasoning behind each beat, useful as a reusable scripting checklist.
