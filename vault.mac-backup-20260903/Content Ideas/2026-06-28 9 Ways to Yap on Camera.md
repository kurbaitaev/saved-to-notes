# 9 Ways to Yap on Camera

> casual on camera, calculated in the message
> — Tom Boyd

**Useful for:** Anyone building a talking-head video presence and wanting to improve delivery, structure, and hooks.

## Key points
- Start mid-thought — no one needs the setup
- Make people feel seen OR help people see — do one or the other
- Awkward hands → hold something; a drink works
- If you're new, start with lists of three — easiest place to start
- Casual but calculated: casual on camera, calculated in the message
- Human mind for ideas, AI to organize
- Ask yourself 'so what?' — if the answer is obviously, you don't have a point
- Script, outline, or go off the top — do what works for you
- The hook = dialed copy + energy, equal parts of both
- Study creators with under 10K followers and look at their outlier videos
- Create a folder of favorite yap videos and study their first three seconds and text hooks
- Apply the 'I believe' test: someone can watch your video and know exactly what you believe
- Change the language in videos you watch to focus on tonality, delivery, and cadence instead of the words
- Disregard advice that doesn't work for you — the goal is volume so you can see patterns

**Why save:** Dense, practical framework for on-camera talking-head video with actionable techniques for structure, delivery, and hooks.
