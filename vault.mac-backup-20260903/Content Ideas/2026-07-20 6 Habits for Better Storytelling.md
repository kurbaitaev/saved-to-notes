# 6 Habits for Better Storytelling

**Main idea:** Build storytelling instincts through six daily micro-habits that train observation, memory, improvisation, and on-camera delivery.

## Key points
- Screenshot any text that made you feel something into one album — your storytelling bank so you never run out of material
- Write down every person you meet and occasionally narrate their story to yourself, practicing turning real people into stories repeatedly
- Photograph specific small details (a design on a building, a crack on the sidewalk) — not wide shots — to train your eye for the details that make stories come alive
- Pick a random word each day and tell yourself a one-minute story about it so you can turn anything into a story on the spot
- Flip your phone face down and listen to a video of yourself to analyze your tone, rhythm, and inflection
- Watch the same video on mute to study your facial expressions and body language separately

**Why save:** Six concrete, non-obvious habits that build storytelling instinct across observation, memory, improvisation, and delivery — actionable without any tools.
