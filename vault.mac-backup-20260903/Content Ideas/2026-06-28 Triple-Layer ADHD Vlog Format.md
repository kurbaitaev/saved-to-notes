# Triple-Layer ADHD Vlog Format

> Формат, который принесет вам буквально тысячи подписчиков.

**Main idea:** A triple-layer Instagram video format — process clip on top, talking head in the middle, process clip on the bottom — drives massive reach and can be used for any vlog content.

## Key points
- Layout: process/activity footage on top, talking-head clip in the middle, process/activity footage on the bottom — all playing simultaneously
- Originally used by a creator as a YouTube vlog teaser; it went viral, so she adopted it for standalone Instagram vlogs
- Works for any vlog topic: day-in-the-life, what I eat in a day, travel, etc.
- Described as an 'ADHD format' — multiple visual layers running at once keep viewers engaged

**Why save:** Gives a concrete, immediately replicable video layout that reportedly drives large follower growth on Instagram.
