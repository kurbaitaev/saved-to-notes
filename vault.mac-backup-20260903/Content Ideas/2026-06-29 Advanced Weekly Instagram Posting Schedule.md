# Advanced Weekly Instagram Posting Schedule

**Main idea:** A balanced weekly mix of effort levels across reel lengths and carousels is all you need to satisfy the algorithm and grow without overcomplicating your content calendar.
**Useful for:** Content creators who want a structured, effort-balanced weekly posting plan for Instagram growth.

## Steps
1. 1 medium-effort 30-second reel per week
2. 1-2 shareable carousels per week
3. 1 higher-effort 1-minute+ signature series per week
4. 1-2 lower-effort 7-15 second reels per week
5. 1 higher-effort 1-minute+ reel per week

**Why save:** Gives a concrete, effort-tiered weekly posting framework designed to keep the algorithm happy without burning out.
