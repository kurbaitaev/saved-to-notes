# Pitch Brands Like a Marketer

> pitch brands like a strategic marketer, not a fan
> — itshannahzhang

**Useful for:** Creators who want to land brand deals by presenting a business case instead of a fan letter

## Key points
- Brand deals come out of a marketing team's budget — they need a business case, not enthusiasm
- Audience fit beats follower count every time
- Show proof your content converts with real case studies and stats from past partnerships

## Steps
1. Define who your audience is and explain exactly why they are the customers that brand wants to reach
2. Prove your content converts — include case studies and stats from past partnerships
3. Pitch 2-3 specific content ideas built for that brand so they can picture the campaign before spending a dollar
