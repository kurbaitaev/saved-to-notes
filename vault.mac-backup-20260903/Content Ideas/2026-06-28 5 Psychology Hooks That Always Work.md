# 5 Psychology Hooks That Always Work

> It took me 10 years to learn this but I'll teach it to you in less than one minute

**Main idea:** Five psychological hooks that reliably capture and hold viewer attention on short-form video.

## Key points
- Challenge hook — 'Is it possible to [specific challenge]?' turns the video into a story with unresolved tension; the viewer stays to close the loop
- Time compression hook — 'It took me X years, I'll teach you in 1 minute' triggers FOMO by making skipping feel like discarding years of experience
- Specific results hook — 'What makes [subject] so [specific result]?' frames a case study and implies authority, viewer stays to learn the secret
- Question hook — 'How long do you think you have to [action] to [result]?' forces the viewer to guess, creating an open loop they need resolved
- The word 'psychology' alone signals authority and raises attention — it is itself a hook
