# FUEL Framework for Content Topics

> Your life is content. What you did today. What you're building. Every single thought you have is content.
> — austingeorgas

**Main idea:** The FUEL acronym gives content creators a ready-made framework for never running out of things to say on camera.
**Useful for:** Anyone starting to post video content who doesn't know what to talk about.

## Key points
- F — First person: your daily life, purchases, businesses, and thoughts are all content.
- U — Unpopular: say the things your audience thinks but won't say themselves — that's how you stand out.
- E — Experiences: mine past struggles, challenges, and ended friendships for lessons others can learn from.
- L — Listen: once you're posting, your comment section tells you exactly what topics to cover next.

**Why save:** Compact, memorable acronym that solves the most common beginner blocker in video content creation.
