# Top 5 LinkedIn Formats for Founders

> You can't start a post saying hot take and then give a lukewarm take.
> — dibratto

**Main idea:** Five LinkedIn content formats that consistently drive high impressions for tech founders and CEOs.
**Takeaway:** Tweet reacts and monthly recaps are underused by founders but are performing strongly on LinkedIn right now.

## Key points
- Origin story: where you were before, the lightbulb moment, the early days, and where you are now.
- Tweet react: find a relevant tweet and add your take — agree or disagree, but justify it.
- Industry news commentary: share a headline your ICP would care about, then add expert opinion.
- Controversial opinion: only works if at least 50% of your space would disagree with it — no lukewarm hot takes.
- Monthly recap: new hires, ARR growth, new features or service lines — positives are enough.

**Why save:** Comes from someone ghostwriting LinkedIn content at 500k–1M impressions per week for tech CEOs — not theoretical advice.
