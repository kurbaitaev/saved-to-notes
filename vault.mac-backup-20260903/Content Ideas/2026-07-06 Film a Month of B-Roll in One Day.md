# Film a Month of B-Roll in One Day

**Useful for:** Content creators who want to batch-shoot B-roll once and reuse clips for weeks instead of constantly reshooting.

## Steps
1. Start with a plan: make a list of repeatable, useful shots you can film in one space (working at your desk, drinking coffee, reading or journaling, typing on a laptop, making tea, scrolling on your phone) — build a personal stock library from your real life.
2. Shoot wide, medium, and tight: film every scene in 3 ways — close-ups (add detail and curiosity), mid shots (show emotion and action), wide shots (set the scene). Combine all 3 for smoother edits and better flow.
3. Repeat the action: choose a simple action, break it into 3–5 steps, and reshoot each step from multiple angles (overhead, POV, low-angle) — this gives you enough footage to stitch a mini story together in post.
4. Film your real life: turn everyday tasks into content — your routine (breakfast, journaling, workouts), your process (editing, brainstorming, behind the scenes), personal moments (books, meditation, slow mornings). Let people into your world, it builds trust fast.
5. Don't forget the stills: while the angle is set up, take an extra 60 seconds to set a self-timer and get a still of the same setup — keeps your carousels and thumbnails on-brand.
6. Organize for reuse: create labeled folders on your laptop or phone, back up on external drives, reuse clips across reels, carousels, and shorts — consistency gets easier when you've already got the footage.

**Why save:** A concrete 6-step system for batch-filming B-roll that reduces daily shooting overhead and builds a reusable personal stock library.
