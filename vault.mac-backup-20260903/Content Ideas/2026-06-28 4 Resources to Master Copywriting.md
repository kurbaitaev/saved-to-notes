# 4 Resources to Master Copywriting

> Marketing is the only skill set you need in the 21st century.


## Key points
- Align your marketing message with the thinking patterns of the people you're trying to reach and direct them toward what you're selling
- A confused mind will never buy — all copy needs one core message (rule of one)
- Great copywriting makes clients feel understood, so they seek you out
- One good ad can generate millions in sales; refining a single ad is worth months of work
