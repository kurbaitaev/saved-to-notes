# LinkedIn Growth Fundamentals Checklist

**Main idea:** Consistent LinkedIn reach comes from posting frequently, engaging outbound daily, and reviewing what works — no hacks or engagement pods needed.

## Key points
- Post 3–5 times per week
- Use more founder pictures when relevant
- Write more things that AI cannot write
- Leave 15 outbound comments per day
- Send 20 outbound connections per day
- Review content weekly: track which angles, formats, hooks, and media are working
