# 30 Hooks for Talk-to-Camera Reels

**Main idea:** 30 ready-to-use opening hook lines specifically for talk-to-camera Reels, grouped across three slides.

## Key points
- I wish someone had told me this sooner.
- This changed the way I look at __ forever.
- I think we've been doing this completely wrong.
- I wish more people were talking about this.
- I learned this lesson the hard way.
- I used to believe this too… until I realized this.
- Nobody tells you this part.
- This is probably why you're still stuck.
- I honestly think this is where most people go wrong.
- Here's the shift that changed everything for me.
- If you're struggling with __, stop what you're doing and listen.
- This might be the advice you need to hear today.
- I didn't believe this either at first.
- Let me save you months of frustration.
- Here's what finally clicked for me.
- I used to think this was the answer… it wasn't.
- You're asking the wrong question.
- I can almost guarantee you're overlooking this.
- This is exactly what I'd do if I had to start over today.
- The biggest breakthrough I had wasn't strategy, it was this.
- Everyone is focused on the wrong thing.
- I wish people would stop telling you to do this.
- The internet made you believe this… and it's hurting you.
- This is the conversation nobody in this industry is having.
- If this sounds like you, keep listening.
- I don't think this gets talked about enough.
- Here's the truth most people aren't ready to hear.
- This one realization made everything easier.
- Before you post another piece of content, hear me out.
- This is the advice I'd give every beginner.

**Why save:** A plug-and-play bank of 30 proven talk-to-camera opening hooks that trigger curiosity and stop the scroll.
