# Old Instagram vs New Instagram

> let go of what you can't control, and focus on what you can grow.
> — thecreative.social

**Main idea:** Instagram strategy has shifted — creators who win now use format diversity, value-first profiles, algorithm metrics (watch time, shares), evergreen content, and collabs instead of solo posting.

## Key points
- Content format: use the 40/30/20 rule — reels for reach (40%), carousels for depth (30%), stories for sales (20%); each format gets ONE job.
- Profile aesthetic: value > beauty — a clear profile that says what you do wins over a pretty grid with weak content.
- What to measure: shares (4–5x weight) and watch time matter far more than likes (1x) to the algorithm.
- Content shelf life: aim for evergreen content that lasts weeks to months; Instagram now reads captions, on-screen text, and audio to surface it.
- Account growth: do 2 collabs a month instead of growing alone — a whole new crowd finds you.

**Why save:** Concise side-by-side breakdown of outdated vs. current Instagram tactics across five key creator levers.
