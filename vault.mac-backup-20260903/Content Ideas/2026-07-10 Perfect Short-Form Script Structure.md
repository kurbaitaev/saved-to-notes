# Perfect Short-Form Script Structure

> hook lead body one open loop one body two open loop two body three cta this is the perfect structure for short form content
> — realskytan

**Main idea:** A 7-part scripting template — Hook, Lead, Body, Open Loop (×2), and CTA — maximizes retention and converts the most engaged viewers.

## Key points
- Hook breaks the pattern of what viewers see in the feed
- Lead pulls viewers to the 10-second bar so they stop scrolling
- Body 1 introduces the first piece of value
- Open Loop 1 creates curiosity to keep watching (e.g. 'yet that's not the craziest part')
- Body 2 delivers a second piece of value
- Open Loop 2 adds another curiosity spike (e.g. a surprising fact or unexpected twist)
- Body 3 delivers the final piece of value
- CTA takes the warmest viewers to the next step (e.g. 'comment [keyword] to get value')
- Repeat the Body + Open Loop structure twice before the CTA

**Why save:** Gives a concrete, repeatable 7-part script template that addresses both retention and conversion for short-form video.
