# 5 Ways to Be Great Creator

**Main idea:** Five concrete habits that separate great content creators from average ones, from a creator with 1.3M followers.
**Takeaway:** Entertain and inspire first before you educate — and don't care when a video flops.

## Key points
- Save outlier reels from your favorite creators to study what breaks patterns.
- When a movie, show, or event goes viral, figure out how to link that topic to your niche.
- Entertain and inspire first before you educate.
- Take currently viral formats and make them more authentically yours.
- Don't care when a video flops — it's evidence you're trying and experimenting.
