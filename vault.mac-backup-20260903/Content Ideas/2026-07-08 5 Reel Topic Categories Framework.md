# 5 Reel Topic Categories Framework

**Main idea:** Reels ideas fall into five topic categories that cover different audience needs and reach goals.
**Takeaway:** Use all five topic types — personal, expert, general, trigger, and pain-point — to cover every reach angle.

## Key points
- Personal topic
- Expert topic
- General topic
- Trigger topic — designed for high reach
- Topic that closes pain points of your target audience

**Why save:** Compact five-category framework for generating Reel ideas that balance personal brand, authority, reach, and audience relevance.
