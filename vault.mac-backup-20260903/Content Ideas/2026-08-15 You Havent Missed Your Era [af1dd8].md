# You Haven't Missed Your Era

> People with less experience and credentials are having more impact than you. So now you refuse to miss the yap era, the green screen era, the put the camera on your face, share your wisdom wherever you are in the kitchen, in the car era, the knowledge creator era. If that's you, welcome.
> — bonusfootage

**Main thought:** Everyone who feels like they missed the blog, vlog, course, TikTok, and podcast waves now has one last open door: the knowledge creator era, where you just show up on camera and share what you know.
**Takeaway:** The wave you haven't missed yet is the one where you put a camera on your face and speak.

## Key points
- Creator FOMO is real — blog era, YouTube vlog era, course era, TikTok era, podcast era all feel 'missed'
- The current moment (knowledge creator / yap era) is framed as the accessible, still-open window
- The barrier is low: kitchen, car, wherever you are — no studio, no credentials required

**Why save:** A sharp rallying-cry hook that reframes creator FOMO as motivation — useful as a content idea or caption reference for the 'it's not too late' angle.
