# Batch a Full Month of Content

> I create a full month of content in just one day
> — neshawoolery

**Useful for:** Creators who want to stop creating daily and instead batch all content in one or two dedicated sessions per week or month

## Key points
- Two schedules: Schedule 1 (post 4x/week) = batch everything once a month; Schedule 2 (post 1-2x/day) = batch weekly
- Two things make batching work: reusable templates (scripts, captions, hooks) and a mix of high-effort and low-effort content
- High-effort = talking to camera; low-effort = b-roll reels with text on top — you need both or you won't finish the batch day
- If posting 2x/day: film everything on batch day, then spend just 1 hour each morning editing — you're not creating from scratch daily

## Steps
1. 8am — Brainstorm reposts + new content ideas for the week
2. 9am — Schedule your reposts for the week
3. 11am — Script all videos with Poppy AI
4. 2pm — Film everything
5. 3:30pm — Edit. If posting once a day, edit all videos now and you're done. If posting 2x/day, move to the daily editing block instead
6. Daily (8am–9am) — Edit and schedule 1-2 videos in CapCut. Everything is already filmed — you're just finishing what you started
