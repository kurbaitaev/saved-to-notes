# 33 Negative Hooks That Boost Reach

**Main idea:** Negativity stops the scroll — 33 fill-in-the-blank negative hook templates organized by type to boost content reach.

## Key points
- Stop [action] if you actually want to [dream result].
- Here are [#] [noun] as a [title] I would never own.
- How to stop [action] for good.
- If you don't have [item/action], do [item/action].
- If you [action] like this, then you're doing it wrong.
- Don't make the mistake of [action], [action], [action].
- Why does nobody talk about the cost of [action].
- [#] things you should never put in your [noun].
- Don't start doing [action] until you learn how to do this.
- [#] things you should never put in the [noun].
- It's time to throw away your [item]. You don't need it.
- [#] days/months/years ago I quit [habit].
- Never [action] first and then [action].
- Stop buying your [noun] like this, and start buying them like this.
- [#] years as a [occupation], and you still don't believe me.
- If you want to [dream result], [action] is the worst thing you can do.
- Here's why I never use a [noun].
- If you've ever secretly [action] and hope no one noticed this is for you.
- If I were in my [age range] here is exactly how I would avoid [bad result].
- Ranking the most popular [noun] from worst to best.
- The worst [noun] that you will regret buying for your [noun] is right here.
- I am going to show you how to [verb] your [noun] so they don't [pain point].
- They said, "[famous cliché or quote]" That's a lie.
- My [noun] never [pain point] no matter what I do.
- To everyone just doing [#] [action]. Stop that.
- Your life is boring because you don't [action].
- There's probably a reason why most people don't [action].
- They say you can't [result] with [noun] but they're wrong.
- [noun] used to have [trait], now they don't.
- If you don't have [thing], do [thing] instead.
- It's time to throw away your [item], you don't need it anymore.
- You wouldn't get [bad result] if you just [action].
- They say you can't [result] with [noun]. They're wrong.

**Why save:** A ready-to-use bank of 33 fill-in-the-blank negative hook templates, organized into five categories (stop, why, mistake, never, callout), that can be applied to any niche to stop the scroll.
