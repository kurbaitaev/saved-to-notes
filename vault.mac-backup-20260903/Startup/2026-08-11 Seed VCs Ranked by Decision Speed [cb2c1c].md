## Post text

The average seed round takes 12.5 weeks to close. Some funds decide in a day.

Ranked by documented speed, fastest first:

1. @ycombinator: YC. Ten-minute interview, answer the same day. A call if yes, an email if no. Still the benchmark everyone else gets measured against. $500K standard deal.

2. @felicis: Felicis. Their own homepage: "often first meeting to term sheet in 24 hours." They once wrote a term sheet on a napkin for Notion. $900M new fund, leads from seed to Series C.

3. @HustleFundVC: Hustle Fund. One 30-60 minute call, typical decision within 48 hours. The GP you meet is the one who decides, no committee behind the curtain. $150K first checks.

4. @kimaventures: Kima. Backs two startups a week, answers in days. €150K fixed ticket, any sector, no negotiation. Over 1,200 companies and counting.

5. Right Side Capital (rightsidecapital dot com, not really on X): first response in two business days, yes or no in about a week, no warm intro needed. $150-400K checks, and they tell you why when they pass. 2,000+ investments.

6. Correlation Ventures (correlationvc dot com): decisions within days, but here's the catch: they never lead. Once someone prices your round, they're the fastest yes for the rest of it.

7. @tylercowen: Emergent Ventures. Not equity, grants. One 1,500-word application, read personally by Tyler Cowen, decision inside two weeks. The lowest-bureaucracy money in the world.

8. @BoostVC: Boost VC. Next steps within a week of the first call, final decision a week or two later. $500K pre-seed checks, leads.

Bling Capital and Soma Capital are famously fast too. Neither publishes a number. If you're reading this, let us know 👀

Save this for the raise. Twelve weeks is a choice, not a law.
