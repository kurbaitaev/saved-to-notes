## Post text

If you're under 25 and building something, there's more money for you than you think.

11 places that write checks to students and dropouts, with the actual terms:

1. @thielfellowship: Thiel Fellowship. $250K over two years to build instead of finishing your degree. Not $100K, that number is two raises old. Vitalik, Dylan Field of Figma and Luminar's Austin Russell all came through here.

2. @1517fund: 1517 Fund. The two people who ran the Thiel Fellowship now run a $175M fund for dropouts and students. $50K at idea stage, up to $1M. Wrote the first check into Loom.

3. @Neo: Neo. Ali Partovi gives ~30 students a year $25K, equity free, to take a gap semester and build in SF. One of his scholars was an 18-year-old MIT kid named Michael Truell. Partovi later wrote the first check into his company, Cursor.

4. @zfellows: Z Fellows. $10K for one week, invested at a $1B cap, so you keep basically everything. High schoolers welcome, no need to quit anything. Cursor, Cognition and Etched founders are alumni.

5. @DormRoomFund: Dorm Room Fund. $40K checks decided entirely by student partners. Just raised its largest fund ever. You need at least one founder still enrolled or within a year of graduating.

6. @pearvc: Pear Competition. Runs every semester, and winning gets you $100K on an uncapped SAFE, the friendliest instrument in venture. Any US or Canadian student, idea stage is fine.

7. @generalcatalyst: GC Venture Fellows. $25K first checks sourced by 20 student fellows across campuses. Used to be called Rough Draft Ventures. Their picks went on to raise $4B+.

8. @tylercowen: Emergent Ventures. Tyler Cowen personally reads short applications from anyone 13 and up, worldwide, and decides fast. Over 1,000 grants so far, several to teenagers.

9. @776foundation: 776 Fellowship. Alexis Ohanian's version of the Thiel Fellowship: $100K over two years, ages 18-24, but climate only.

10. @ycombinator: YC Early Decision. Apply while still in school, get the $500K, defer the batch until after graduation. The anti-dropout option, launched last fall.

11. @fdotinc: Founders, Inc. Six-week summer program at Fort Mason for students and recent grads. Top teams get up to $250K. This year's cohort is running right now.

Save this, or send it to the smartest 19-year-old you know.
