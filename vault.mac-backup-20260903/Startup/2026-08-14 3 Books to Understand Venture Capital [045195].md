## Transcript

Here are three books to learn more about the venture capital industry. Number one, VC in American history. Most people think the venture capital industry started in Silicon Valley, but it actually started with whaling ships in the 1800s. These used to be super risky voyages that were either highly profitable or fatal, and the book goes over the entire arc of going from whaling ships to where it is today. Number two, zero to one by Peter Thiel. Peter Thiel is one of the greatest investors of our generation, and in this book, he goes over what it takes to think like an entrepreneur and build a great company. And I think it's such a great read because if you want to understand venture, you need to understand what it takes to build a great startup. Number three, the power law. This book gives you direct access to the people running Sequoia, Kleiner Perkins, Excel, and SoftBank, and it really shows you the math behind this industry. It's the closest thing to sitting in the actual room and understanding how the best investors think.
