# Z Fellows One-Week Silicon Valley Program

**Main idea:** Z Fellows is a one-week program that gives early builders $10,000 and direct access to founders of billion-dollar companies in Silicon Valley.

## Key points
- 1 week in Silicon Valley with founders of multi-billion-dollar companies
- $10,000 grant (optional equity investment at a $1B valuation cap)
- Designed for early-stage builders, students, and side-project founders
- Applications are a written form plus a 1-minute personal video (not a product demo)
- Cohorts run roughly every other month
- Ongoing support continues for the life of your company after the week ends

- [ ] ✅ [Z Fellows](https://www.zfellows.com/)

**Why save:** A rare paid one-week program that puts early-stage builders in direct contact with billion-dollar founders — worth tracking for future application windows.
