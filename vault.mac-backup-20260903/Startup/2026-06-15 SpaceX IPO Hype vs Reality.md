# SpaceX IPO Hype vs Reality

> Profit profit and Elon Musk, that's like oil and water.
> — stilleaworkinprogress

**Main thought:** A mother-and-family conversation arguing that SpaceX's $1.75 trillion IPO valuation is driven by hype around unproven future revenues — not actual profit.
**Takeaway:** A company planning to go public at a higher valuation than Amazon, Berkshire Hathaway, and Meta has never turned a single penny of profit.

## Key points
- Starlink is SpaceX's primary revenue source — but revenue is not the same as profit
- SpaceX has never made a net profit
- The planned IPO would value SpaceX at $1.75 trillion — 7th most valuable company in the world on day one
- That valuation exceeds Amazon, Berkshire Hathaway, and Meta
- The hype centers on satellite data centers and human missions to the moon and Mars
- People on the moon or Mars will not generate profit in our lifetime
- Satellite data centers beaming information from orbit could be valuable — but it has not been done yet
- Parallels to Elon's robots-in-every-household-by-2025 promise that also did not materialize

**Why save:** Concise plain-language explainer of why SpaceX's IPO valuation is a bet on unproven future revenue rather than demonstrated profitability.
