# Operator Angels 16–20 + DM Playbook


## Key points
- Angels write personal cheques up to $100K — no fund gatekeeping, faster decisions
- Warm up 10–14 days: engage on their posts before any DM; reply rate jumps 4–5x
- First DM is 3 lines max — what you're building (specific), why now (market timing), why them (reference a portfolio company); no deck in message 1
- Lead with one metric: '$30K MRR in 8 weeks' beats 'we're building the future of X'
- DM within 30 min of their post — they're online, reply rate triples
- Close with 'no reply needed if it's not a fit' — removes pressure, increases response rate
- Follow up once after 7 days, then move on; multi-follow-ups signal weakness
