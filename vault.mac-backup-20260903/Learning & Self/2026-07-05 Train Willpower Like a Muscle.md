# Train Willpower Like a Muscle

**Main idea:** Willpower can be trained like a muscle — a review of 63 studies found physical exercise and mindfulness show the strongest effects.

## Key points
- 4 training types studied: physical exercise, cognitive training, mindfulness, video games
- Exercise and mindfulness showed the strongest effects on willpower
- The more you train willpower, the more efficient it becomes
- Electrically stimulating the anterior mid-cingulate cortex also appears to increase willpower (not a DIY method)
