# Athletic and Aesthetic Full Masterclass

**Useful for:** Anyone who wants to build an aesthetic physique while staying fast, strong, and conditioned — not just big.

## Steps
1. NUTRITION — Find maintenance: track food for 3–7 days and take the weekly average.
2. NUTRITION — Decide goal: fat loss if abs aren't visible; muscle gain if already lean.
3. NUTRITION — Set weekly calorie target at ±10% of maintenance (deficit for cut, surplus for muscle).
4. NUTRITION — Training days: protein 1g/lb BW, fats 0.6–1.0 g/kg BW (low end on cut, high end on bulk), carbs fill the rest.
5. NUTRITION — Non-training days: drop calories 10–20%, cut carbs in half, fill remainder with fats.
6. TRAINING — Start every session with some combination of plyometrics, sprinting, and lifting.
7. TRAINING — Lift close to failure: 1–3 sets per exercise, 5–15 reps.
8. TRAINING — Cover these movement patterns each session: squat, hinge, horizontal push, horizontal pull, vertical push, vertical pull, arms/laterals/leg extensions/leg curls.
9. TRAINING — Add conditioning 1–2 times per week for an unlimited gas tank.
10. TRACKING — Weigh yourself daily and take progress photos every week to adjust.
