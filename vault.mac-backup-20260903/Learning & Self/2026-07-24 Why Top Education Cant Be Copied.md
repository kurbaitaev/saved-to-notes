# Why Top Education Can't Be Copied

> You can have the best pedagogical methods in the world on paper, but if it doesn't align with the cultural and contextual realities, it's not going to work.
> — unjadedjade

**Main idea:** Top-ranked education systems cannot be simply imported into other countries because the cultural values around effort, intelligence, and teaching are impossible to transplant.

## Key points
- Western cultures tend to view academic ability as innate ('I'm just not a maths person'); many Asian cultures genuinely believe success is merely the result of effort and persistence.
- England's Shanghai Maths programme trained teachers in new methods but couldn't import the culture of growth mindset that makes those methods effective.
- When given an unsolvable problem, Japanese students grew more motivated to try harder; Canadian students assumed they were the problem and didn't want to continue.
- How teachers are paid and respected in society (Singapore vs. UK) is a contextual factor just as important as pedagogical method.
