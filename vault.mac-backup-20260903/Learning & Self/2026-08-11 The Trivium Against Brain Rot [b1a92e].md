# The Trivium Against Brain Rot

> The muscle you don't use is the one you lose.
> — hangxw

**Main idea:** The Trivium — an ancient framework of grammar, logic, and rhetoric — offers a 3-step antidote to information overload: consume in small doses, reflect by connecting ideas to your own life, then paraphrase everything into your own words.

## Key points
- Human knowledge now doubles every 12 hours, but our ability to process it keeps shrinking
- Saving posts and consuming summaries at 2x speed creates the illusion of understanding, not real understanding
- Plato warned over 2,000 years ago that relying too much on outside tools weakens our ability to think — AI accelerates the same problem
- If you can't explain a concept using your own stories and examples, you don't fully understand it yet

## Steps
1. Consume in small doses — 15 minutes max — then stop and write down what you remember
2. Take notes on how you feel about it and connect it to something from your own life (a book, a conversation, a pattern you keep seeing)
3. Paraphrase all of it into your own words using your own stories and examples
