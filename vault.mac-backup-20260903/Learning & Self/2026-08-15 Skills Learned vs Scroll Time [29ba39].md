# Skills Learned vs Scroll Time

> Skills you could've learned instead of scrolling
> — oliviastudies5

**Main idea:** The hours spent scrolling each month are enough to learn real skills — Rubik's cube at 30 hrs, world capitals at 90, constellations at 150, hieroglyphics at 240.

## Key points
- 30 hours/month scrolling → enough to learn to solve a Rubik's cube
- 90 hours/month scrolling → enough to learn every country's capital (Quizlet, 198 flashcards)
- 150 hours/month scrolling → enough to learn astronomy / star constellations (at ~5 hours/week)
- 240 hours/month scrolling → enough time to start understanding hieroglyphics
