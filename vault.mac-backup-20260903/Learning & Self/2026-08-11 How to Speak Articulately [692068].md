# How to Speak Articulately

> People who are articulate don't think faster than you, they think before you.
> — kolbykirschner

**Main idea:** Articulation is built before you speak, not in the moment — through silent mental incubation (walks) followed by immediate free-writing.
**Takeaway:** Give your ideas silence to finish forming, then write them out immediately after.

## Key points
- Articulate people think before they speak, not faster — the real skill is pre-speech processing
- Constant content consumption (scrolling, podcasts, music) prevents ideas from fully forming
- Unstructured mental downtime lets the brain consolidate thoughts and access insight

## Steps
1. Take a 30-minute morning walk — no podcasts, music, or content, just silence
2. Take a 30-minute evening walk — same rule, no input
3. Sit down immediately after each walk and write freely until you have nothing left to write
