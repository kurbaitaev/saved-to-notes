# Harness Neuroplasticity for Your Goals

> There is nothing in this world you can't achieve with the power of neuroplasticity on your side.
> — kimberlyydang

**Main idea:** Neuroplasticity lets you intentionally rewire your neural pathways to achieve personal and professional goals by controlling your inputs and behaviors.
**Takeaway:** Cut negative inputs, shift to identity-based self-talk, stack habits, and train without distractions — your brain will follow.

## Key points
- Eliminate negative self-talk, sad music, and self-destructive habits that don't align with your goals
- Shift from outcome-based to identity-based self-talk (e.g., 'I'm a person who prioritises strength and nutrition')
- Habit stack: attach a new behavior to an existing one (e.g., pack your gym bag before sleep)
- Mindful training: no phone or Instagram Reels during workouts
