# 3 Hacks to Beat Doom Scrolling

**Main idea:** Three practical habits to fight doom scrolling: leave the house, block apps with Brick, and plan your day the night before with a checklist.

## Key points
- Leave the house — staying home creates comfort that makes all-day scrolling easy; being out keeps you moving and less phone-focused
- Write a nightly to-do list (checklist) the night before to avoid an aimless day; checking off tasks removes the overwhelm that triggers doom scrolling

## Recommended

- [ ] ✅ [Notion](https://www.notion.so) — Notion Labs
