# Compress 12 Months Into 12 Weeks

**Main idea:** Three high-leverage habits — quarterly sprint goals, weekly reviews, and morning time blocking — can compress 12 months of progress into 12 weeks.

## Key points
- Set one 12-week sprint goal: pick the one thing that will change everything and give it a single 12-week window — long enough for real progress, short enough to stay locked in
- Do a 15-minute Sunday weekly review: ask what worked, what didn't, and what's next — you can't improve what you don't measure
- Time-block your hardest task first thing in the morning, when your prefrontal cortex is freshest, before the noise kicks in

**Why save:** Compact, immediately actionable framework for accelerating goal achievement using just three repeatable weekly and quarterly habits.
