# Caucasus Literature Reading List

**Main idea:** When reading Caucasus literature, look closely at who is writing, when they wrote, and what perspective they bring.

## Key points
- Caucasus literature is often multilingual, produced across different countries, and spans a wide range of the political spectrum
- These books brought mixed feelings: nostalgia, pride, loss, hope, and survival
- The author does not always agree with every statement made, but found each book enriching and of great value to knowledge, imagination, and sense of belonging
