# Martial Arts Tier List Real Life

> Train with purpose, not hype.
> — jasonyerrington

**Main idea:** A practical tier ranking of martial arts by real-world effectiveness and speed to usable results.

## Key points
- S tier — BJJ: effective across self-defense and competition, results show up quickly in real situations
- S tier — Wrestling: limited move set but highly effective; you get good fast
- A tier — Boxing: fundamental skill, essential to have, but you get rusty without consistent training
- A tier — Judo: similar to wrestling but larger technique library; needs more consistent training to pull together
- A tier — Muay Thai: same format as boxing — necessary, but requires consistent practice
- B tier — Karate: longer commitment to get good, but proven effective (Lyoto Machida's UFC knockouts as evidence)
- C tier — Krav Maga: some good techniques but too conditional (groin strikes, eye gouges) for most situations
- C tier — Kung Fu: good body movement patterns but not broadly applicable
- D tier — Competition-only styles (YouTube martial arts): no real-life value
- D tier — Pressure-point techniques: dismissed entirely as ineffective
