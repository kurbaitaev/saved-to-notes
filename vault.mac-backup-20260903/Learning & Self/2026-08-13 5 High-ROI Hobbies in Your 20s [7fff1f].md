# 5 High-ROI Hobbies in Your 20s

> You can have the money, the fitness, the success, but it doesn't mean anything if you don't know what you're building them for.
> — pedersonperformance


## Key points
- Learn a combat sport (boxing, wrestling, BJJ, Muay Thai) — keeps you fit, teaches calm under pressure, and gives you the ability to protect yourself and others
- Create something — write, make videos, build a business, make music; anything that forces you to think instead of doom scroll
- Read — most people stop learning when school ends; you can absorb decades of someone else's experience for the price of a book
- Invest — in your body, your skills, your relationships; if money is left over after essentials, learn to invest that too because the best investments compound
- Prayer — get quiet, ask God for guidance, figure out what you believe, your calling, and what kind of person you want to become
