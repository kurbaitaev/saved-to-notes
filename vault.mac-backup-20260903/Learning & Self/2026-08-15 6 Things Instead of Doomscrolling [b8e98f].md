# 6 Things Instead of Doomscrolling

**Main idea:** Six concrete activities to replace mindless doomscrolling, ranging from intentional online learning to offline physical activity.

## Key points
- 1. Learn on Substack
- 2. Read a book
- 3. Art of coffee
- 4. Board games
- 5. Walks in nature
- 6. Run (no targets)

## Transcript

10 out of 10 things to do instead of scrolling to prevent brain rot. Go on sub stack and learn a new topic, psychology, philosophy, neuroscience. Dive into new topics that you're interested in, read a book, fiction, nonfiction, wherever it is you'd like. Don't worry about trying to learn, but read slowly with intention, absorb what you'll read in. Learn the art of coffee. Coffee is actually surprisingly hard to make, especially a good cup of coffee, and it's even harder to learn how to do coffee art. But it's a skill and it's better than doom scrolling. Play ball games with your friends, your girlfriend, boyfriend. This is one of my favorite things to do is buying a new ball game and playing it with friends. It's just so fun to do. Go for a walk in nature, go to a place you've never been before. Go and climb a small hill and take in the view. Go for a run early morning during the day, late at night, just taking your surroundings, no music, go with a friend and just enjoy it with no targets in mind.
