# Brain Needs Repetition to Learn

> your brain needs repetition. It needs time to process and retain information.

**Main idea:** Spaced study sessions with breaks outperform cramming because the brain needs repeated exposure and downtime to consolidate information.

## Key points
- Cramming into one day is ineffective — the brain needs to see material again the next day to reinforce it
- Breaks between study sessions let the brain download and store information
- Revisiting material on subsequent days strengthens retention
- Studying in one overly long stretch causes you to lose information

**Why save:** Clear, plain-language explanation of why spaced practice beats cramming, useful for anyone building a study habit.
