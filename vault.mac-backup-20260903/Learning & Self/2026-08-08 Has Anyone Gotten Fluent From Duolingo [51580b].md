# Has Anyone Gotten Fluent From Duolingo

> has anyone actually become fluent from Duolingo
> — arziparzi

**Context:** Creator questions out loud whether Duolingo actually produces fluency, while the caption admits they keep using it only out of fear of losing their streak.
**Main thought:** People may use Duolingo not for real language learning but to avoid the anxiety of losing their streak.
**Takeaway:** Maintaining a streak is not the same as making actual progress.

**Why save:** Captures the gap between gamified habit-keeping and genuine skill-building in language learning apps.

**Original:** https://vt.tiktok.com/ZS4pajAgM/
