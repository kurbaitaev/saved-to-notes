# 5 Books for Career Clarity

**Main idea:** Five books that teach how to think about your career — prioritizing skill-building, experimentation, and purpose over passion-chasing.

## Key points
- Skills matter more than finding your passion
- Feeling lost in your 20s is more common than you think — don't mistake uncertainty for failure
- Passion follows mastery, not the other way around
- Treat your career like an experiment instead of a lifelong decision
- Your best career is often where love, skill, world need, and pay overlap

## Recommended
- [ ] ✅ [So Good They Can't Ignore You](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/13525945-so-good-they-can-t-ignore-you) — Cal Newport
- [ ] ✅ [The Happiness Curve: Why Life Gets Better After 50](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/34964837-the-happiness-curve) — Jonathan Rauch
- [ ] ✅ [Don't Follow Your Passion, Follow Your Effort](https://blogmaverick.com/2012/03/18/dont-follow-your-passion-follow-your-effort/) — Mark Cuban
- [ ] ✅ [Ikigai: The Japanese Secret to a Long and Happy Life](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/124950497-ikigai) — Héctor García and Francesc Miralles
- [ ] ✅ [Designing Your Life: How to Build a Well-Lived, Joyful Life](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/26046333-designing-your-life) — Bill Burnett and Dave Evans
