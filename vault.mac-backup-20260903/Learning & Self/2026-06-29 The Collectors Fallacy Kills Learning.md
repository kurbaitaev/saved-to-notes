# The Collector's Fallacy Kills Learning

> Your brain mistakes storing information for learning it.

**Main idea:** Collecting information creates the illusion of learning; real learning requires active processing and retrieval, not saving.
**Takeaway:** Don't save ideas — turn them into questions you can answer from memory.

## Key points
- Students who typed notes verbatim learned less conceptually than those who paraphrased (Mueller & Oppenheimer, 2014)
- Before saving an idea, ask: Can I explain it without looking? Can I connect it to something I already know? Can I turn it into a question?
- Forcing your brain to retrieve information — not just store it — is what makes learning stick
