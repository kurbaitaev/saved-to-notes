# 3 Ways to Master Anything

> The faster and more effectively you can acquire and learn new skills, the more versatile you'll become.
> — Lauren | Habits & Self Improvement

**Main idea:** Three strategies to excel at anything: meta-learning, cognitive optimization (sleep and nutrition), and active podcast retention using tools like Snipd.

## Key points
- Meta-learning: get good at getting good — the faster you acquire new skills, the more versatile you become across domains
- Optimize cognition: adequate sleep and nutrition are prerequisites that allow your skill-learning capacity to be optimized
- Remember better: the average podcast listener forgets 90% of an episode within the first 48 hours — use tools to actively extract and retain insights
