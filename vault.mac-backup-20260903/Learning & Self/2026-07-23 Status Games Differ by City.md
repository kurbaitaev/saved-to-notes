# Status Games Differ by City

> In the Bay, your net worth is less interesting than the problem you're solving.
> — sorhan.hq

**Main thought:** Each major city runs a different status game — wealth in New York, legacy and institutional proximity in London, what you're building in San Francisco.
**Takeaway:** The city you choose selects the status game you play — pick the one that rewards what you actually do.

## Key points
- New York: wealth defines status — the richer you are, the more powerful
- London: proximity to institutions and old power networks defines status; money alone means little
- San Francisco: what you're building defines status — money and class are irrelevant
- Miami: money and visibility define status
- Boston: academic achievement defines status
- LA: who you know defines status

**Why save:** Reframes city choice as a status-game selection, useful for founders and career builders deciding where to operate.
