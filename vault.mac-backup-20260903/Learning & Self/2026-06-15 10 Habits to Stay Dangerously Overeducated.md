# 10 Habits to Stay Dangerously Overeducated

> Get so good at processing information that an ordinary conversation can't hold you anymore.
> — na.yjun

**Main idea:** A set of progressively niche learning habits designed to sharpen how you acquire, process, and connect knowledge.

## Key points
- Spend an hour a week debating someone smarter than you — debate to get taken apart, not to win.
- Watch a lecture outside your field, then explain it from scratch.
- Put on an educational video (politics, history, anything) while you eat instead of scrolling.
- Explain every idea out loud until you hear where it breaks.
- Keep a running list of things you don't understand yet.
- Read the original instead of someone's summary of it.
- Force two unrelated subjects to connect.
- Get so good at processing information that an ordinary conversation can't hold you anymore.

**Why save:** Concrete, uncommon learning habits that go beyond generic 'read more' advice — useful as a recurring personal audit.
