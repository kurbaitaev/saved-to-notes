# Three Hacks to Fix Attention Span

**Main idea:** Three science-backed micro-habits — verbally labeling distraction urges, glancing at greenery for 40 seconds, and removing your phone from the room — measurably restore and protect attention span.

## Key points
- Name the distraction urge out loud ('distraction impulse') — verbally labeling it reactivates the prefrontal cortex and interrupts the impulse
- A 40-second glance at greenery resets sustained attention — staring out a window at grass or trees is enough
- Phone must be in another room — even face-down on your desk it silently drains focus because part of your brain is actively working to resist looking at it
