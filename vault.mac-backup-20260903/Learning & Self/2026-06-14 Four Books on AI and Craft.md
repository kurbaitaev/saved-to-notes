# Four Books on AI and Craft

Short talking-head reel by @meshtimes recommending four books spanning AI and creativity, algorithmic music, the power behind OpenAI, and software craftsmanship.

## Summary
The creator lists four books worth reading: Creative Machines (AI and human creativity), Mood Machine (Spotify's algorithmic influence on taste), Empire of AI (the people and power driving OpenAI), and Apprenticeship Patterns (practical skill-building for software developers).
