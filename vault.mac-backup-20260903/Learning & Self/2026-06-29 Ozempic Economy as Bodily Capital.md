# Ozempic Economy as Bodily Capital

> The optimization machine has now entered your bloodstream.

**Main thought:** The Ozempic economy reframes body optimization as capital arbitrage — when the economy falters, the body becomes the last convertible asset, and quick fixes like Ozempic, looksmaxxing, and biohacking are just new forms of converting suffering into capital.
**Takeaway:** True optimization might look like subtraction, not adding more interventions that perpetuate self-exploitation.

## Key points
- Han's Achievement Society shifted culture from 'you must' to 'you can,' which inevitably produces burnout through self-optimization
- Brian Johnson's longevity experiment is the Achievement Society medicalized — the optimization machine entering the bloodstream
- Bourdieu's four capitals (economic → cultural → social → symbolic) gain a fifth in the Ozempic economy: bodily capital
- When the economy deteriorates, the body becomes the last convertible asset — so Ozempic, looksmaxxing, and biohacking are forms of capital arbitrage
- Looksmaxxing and bone-smashing are not about attractiveness — they are about converting physical pain and appearance into followers and money
- True optimization may be subtraction, not accumulation of more quick fixes
