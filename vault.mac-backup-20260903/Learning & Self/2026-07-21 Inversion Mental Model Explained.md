# Inversion Mental Model Explained

> When you are so focused on trying to achieve success, you forget about the most important part, and that's how to avoid failure.
> — nanadelrey

**Main idea:** Inversion is a mental model where you outline the opposite of what you want to happen — asking what guarantees failure — to build better plans and challenge hidden assumptions.

## Key points
- Avoiding failure and achieving success are two separate questions that deserve equal weight.
- Ask 'If I were terrible at X, what would I do every day?' — then avoid exactly those things.
- Seek the answer not only from yourself but from people with more or different experience.
- Inversion forces you to challenge your beliefs, not just reinforce them.
