# Delete Duolingo, Use Comprehensible Input

> You've just been defrauded out of your mind by that damn owl.
> — julian_julian

**Main thought:** Duolingo is an ineffective language learning app that wastes your time, money, and effort while making you believe language learning is impossible for you.
**Takeaway:** Delete Duolingo and switch to Stephen Krashen's comprehensible input method — it's how languages are actually acquired.

## Key points
- Duolingo steals your time, effort, and money without producing real fluency
- No one you know actually learned a language on Duolingo — that's the proof
- When Duolingo fails you, it makes you think YOU can't learn languages — but the method is the problem, not you
