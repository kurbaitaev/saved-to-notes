# Curiosity Mapping Monthly Reflection Practice

**Main idea:** Use a monthly mind map to trace where your curiosity has been — start with an event, film, or book that pulled your attention, then branch outward to wherever your mind went.

## Key points
- Start with a central node: an event, something you watched, or something you read that sparked curiosity
- Branch outward from there to wherever your mind went — associations, questions, themes
- No rules; the exercise is personal and entirely your own
- Reflection is at the core of building curiosity
