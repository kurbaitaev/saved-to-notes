# Beginner Electronics to Hackathon Wins

**Useful for:** Beginners in electronics who want to level up from kit projects to winning online contests and hackathons.

## Steps
1. Get a beginner electronics kit (from Amazon, Tata, or any online store) and learn how every component in it works
2. Use Instructables, Hackaday, and Arduino Project Hub to find full tutorials for cool projects and build something that genuinely interests you
3. Enter your build in an online contest — the same sites host competitions you can enter without being in the USA or Europe
