# AI Literacy Is Now Civic Literacy

> it is so deeply important that you understand how ai works even if you don't like it and actually it is more important that you understand how it works if you don't like it
> — carol.yaps

**Main thought:** Understanding AI technically is no longer optional — policy, taxation, and cultural conversations all require it, and non-technical people must be part of those discussions.
**Takeaway:** You cannot meaningfully participate in AI policy debates without understanding the technical mechanics underneath them.

## Key points
- AI automating white-collar jobs could collapse income tax revenue — income tax brings in ~7x more than corporate tax in the US
- One proposed alternative: tax AI agents per human replaced, with companies paying a cut to the government
- Technical problem #1: AI agents are just API calls — geographically flexible, so companies could reroute through low-tax jurisdictions
- Technical problem #2: AI is shifting toward local/on-device models (open-weight), making usage tracking much harder with no shared data-center surface to monitor
- Policy and ethics conversations about AI are now inseparable from understanding the technical mechanics
