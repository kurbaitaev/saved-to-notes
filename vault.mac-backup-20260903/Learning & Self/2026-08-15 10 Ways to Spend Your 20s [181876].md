# 10 Ways to Spend Your 20s

> Start a business with your best friends and don't stop until you can sell it for eight to nine figures or you spend all your money and it fails, but you learned a very valuable lesson.
> — Cam Duty

**Main idea:** A guy about to turn 30 lists the 10 things he'd do in his 20s if he could go back — mixing serious life moves with intentionally provocative picks.

## Key points
- Travel to a medium-risk or high-risk country and vlog it with your best friends.
- Start out in a corporate dungeon just so you can get it out of your system.
- Pick one sport and become insanely good at it.
- Options trading.
- Start a business with your best friends and don't stop until you can sell it for eight to nine figures — or spend all your money and it fails, but you learned a very valuable lesson.
- Go on a ton of vacations with your family or your friends.
- Be the first one in your friend group to have a child.
- Don't say no to nights out, especially before you're 25, because it just gets worse after that.
- Push your endurance to extreme limits and sign up for a 100-mile race or an Ironman.
