## Transcript

10 under 10 logical fallacies, but they get increasingly more niche. Ad homonym, you can't win the argument, so you attack the person making it. Straw man, you twist someone's words into something easier to argue against instead of addressing what they actually said. False dilemma, you present two options as if they're the only ones that exist when there are actually more. Slippery slope, you assume one small decision will automatically lead to the worst possible outcome. Appeal to authority, you believe something is true simply because someone important said it. Genetic fallacy, you reject an idea entirely based on where it came from instead of whether it's actually true. Bandwagon fallacy, you believe something is right because everyone else does. Nirvana fallacy, you reject a perfectly good solution because it isn't completely perfect. Texas sharpshooter, you cherry pick the evidence that supports what you already believe and ignore everything that contradicts. Mott and Bailey, you argue an extreme position until challenged, then retreat to a safe one and pretend that's what you meant, Allah. Follow for more psychology content that challenges everything you believe.
