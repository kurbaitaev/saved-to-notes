# Replace Screen Time with Finance Skills

> 10 out of 10 skills you could have learned based on your screen time.
> — Chase Enloe, CFA

**Main idea:** Replacing daily screen time with deliberate practice of four finance skills — Excel, 10-K reading, Mandarin, and negotiation — compounds into a career-defining edge on Wall Street.

## Key points
- 1 hr/day → master Excel keyboard-only: build a three-statement model from a blank sheet and be the one who structures the deal
- 3 hrs/day → read a 10-K like a menu: spot footnotes, off-balance sheet obligations, and MD&A nuances that 100 analysts scroll past
- 5 hrs/day → reach Mandarin fluency: sit across from sovereign wealth funds and close deals while competitors wait for a translator
- 8 hrs/day → master negotiation: know the other side's number before they say it — requires merger modeling under pressure, covenant analysis, comps and precedent transactions, credit and counterparty risk
