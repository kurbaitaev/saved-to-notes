# Stop Building Knowledge Graveyards

> Stop building graveyards and start actually doing things.
> — erinmerylstudy

**Main idea:** Saving content to lists (Collector's Fallacy) triggers a dopamine reward as if you had actually learned — you haven't — and the unfinished lists then breed chronic mental tension via the Zeigarnik effect.

## Key points
- To-read and to-watch lists feel productive but are just graveyards — they make you feel smarter without actual learning
- Saving a book or article rewards your brain with dopamine as if you had already read it — you haven't
- Long unfinished lists create a low-level tension in the brain (Zeigarnik effect) that makes you feel guilty for not completing them
- It's likely impossible to get through all the lists you've built, yet your brain constantly perceives a gap between what you want to do and what you're doing
