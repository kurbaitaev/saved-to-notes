# Frontal Lobe 4-Page Journal Method

**Useful for:** Anyone who wants a structured daily journaling system to gain clarity on goals, identity, progress, and learning.

## Steps
1. Page 1 — Goals: Write your goals in obsessive detail — what success looks like, what you're working towards, what kind of life you're building
2. Page 2 — Reminders and Affirmations: Write what kind of person you are becoming, what motivates you, and what mindset you want to maintain
3. Page 3 — Proof of Progress: Write what effort you made, what skill you practiced, and what you learned; track daily micro-learning progress during commutes or dead time (creator uses BeFreed for this)
4. Page 4 — Learning Takeaways: Write the exact insight gained, how it connects to your own life, and what you will do differently going forward — be specific, not generic
