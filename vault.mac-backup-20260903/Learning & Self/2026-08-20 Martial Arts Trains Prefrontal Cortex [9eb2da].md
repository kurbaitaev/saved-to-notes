# Martial Arts Trains Prefrontal Cortex

> Every drill where you hold back power, every rep where you check yourself mid-technique, is your brain training that control like a muscle.
> — blake.helps

**Main idea:** Consistent martial arts training physically strengthens the prefrontal cortex, improving executive function and self-control over time.

## Key points
- Holding back power and checking yourself mid-technique during drills is repeated impulse-control exercise that trains the brain's control circuitry.
- Practitioners' calmer stress responses are neurological, not personality — the prefrontal cortex has grown stronger through training.
- The shift isn't 'nothing gets to you' — it's that you have built the capacity to choose your response rather than react.
