# Late Starter Martial Arts Schedule

> If you master your daily schedule, you will have a massive edge on your competition.
> — cyrus.lamotte

**Useful for:** Adult late starters in martial arts who have jobs and responsibilities and can't train full-time.

## Key points
- Step 1 — Morning non-negotiables: Bible/prayer, meal prep, and a strength-conditioning block before your work shift.
- Step 2 — Midday pillars: your 9-to-5, building a side business, and eating properly to fuel evening training.
- Step 3 — Nighttime recovery routine: no big meals 3 hours before bed, no liquids 2 hours before bed, no blue light 1 hour before bed.

## Steps
1. Set 3 morning non-negotiables you do every day before work (e.g. prayer, meal prep, conditioning block).
2. Anchor your day around 3 midday pillars: your job, a side project, and intentional nutrition.
3. Follow a 3-rule nighttime routine to calm your nervous system and maximise recovery.

**Why save:** Practical, concrete 3-step daily schedule framework for adults training martial arts around a job and real-life responsibilities.

## Transcript

If you're a late starter to martial arts, the secret to catching up isn't more training, it's mastering your schedule. As an adult, you can't just eat, sleep, train, and live in the gym simply due to a job, bills, and responsibilities. And trying to go beast mode every day is just going to cause burnout and injuries. So here's a practical three-step solution if you're a late starter trying to manage your schedule. Number one is you need a daily morning routine with three non-negotiables. My non-negotiables are Bible and prayer, meal prepping, and hitting my strength conditioning block before my shift. Number two is you need midday pillars. My three pillars consist of my nine to five lifeguarding job, building my online business, and throughout the day consuming the proper fuel that will help me perform optimally in Judo. Lastly, you need a nighttime routine to calm down your nervous system and prep for recovery. My three-step night routine is no big meals three hours before bed, no liquids two hours before bed, and no blue light one hour before bed. And if you master your daily schedule, you will have a massive edge on your competition.
