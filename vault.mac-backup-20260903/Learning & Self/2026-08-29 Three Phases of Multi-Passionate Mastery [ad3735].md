# Three Phases of Multi-Passionate Mastery

> Rather than doing everything, he did everything worth doing.
> — hangxw

**Main idea:** Multi-passionate people reach mastery not by picking one thing forever, but by moving through three phases — exploring widely, committing to a few survivors, then combining those domains into a unique edge.

## Key points
- Phase 1 — Curiosity: follow every itch and develop taste; valid as a starting point, but the danger is never leaving it
- Phase 2 — Commitment: accept you can't do everything; narrow to 2–3 interests that survived exploration and build depth through reps without visible results; choosing feels like losing options, but depth is the price of entry to phase 3
- Phase 3 — Combination: mastery for multi-passionates comes from crossing domains (psychology + filmmaking, business + storytelling, design + technology), not one-track specialization; the combination is the edge
