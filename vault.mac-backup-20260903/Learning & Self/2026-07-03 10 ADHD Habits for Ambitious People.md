# 10 ADHD Habits for Ambitious People

> The goal isn't to cure your monkey brain. It's to build fences in the jungle.
> — russisbuilding

**Main idea:** Eight evidence-backed habits that reduce ADHD's friction on focus, initiation, and follow-through.

## Key points
- Reduce cognitive load: phone out of the room, tabs closed, desk clean
- Protect working memory: write down tasks immediately before they evaporate
- Use implementation intentions: if situation X, then do Y (e.g., if it's 9am, open laptop; if shoes are on, walk starts)
- Externalize executive function: use calendars, timers, checklists, and reminders instead of relying on memory
- Design for task initiation: pre-load the environment so the first step requires no willpower (clothes laid out, tabs preloaded, gym bag packed)
- Use dopamine strategically: music, movement, sunlight, and quick wins — not doom scrolling or cheap reward loops
- Use body doubling for attentional control: coffee shop, library, or a focus call where the room quietly pressures you to lock in
- Practice metacognition: ask what state you're in, what system is failing, and what cue would help you restart
