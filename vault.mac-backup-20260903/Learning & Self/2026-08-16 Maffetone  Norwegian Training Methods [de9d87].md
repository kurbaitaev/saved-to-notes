# Maffetone & Norwegian Training Methods

> you will run faster and faster at that same exact heart rate
> — Darren Faul | Men's Fitness Coach

**Main idea:** Use the Maffetone Method (180 minus your age as max HR) for easy runs to build aerobic efficiency, and the Norwegian Method for controlled intervals — together they build compounding speed you can actually recover from.

## Key points
- Maffetone Method: take 180 minus your age to get the heart rate ceiling for most of your easy runs
- As aerobic fitness builds over weeks and months, you run faster and faster at that same heart rate
- Norwegian Method: the goal is not to destroy yourself every workout — intervals should be controlled
- Finish each interval session feeling like you had one more rep left in the tank
- One method builds the aerobic base; the other builds speed you can actually recover from
