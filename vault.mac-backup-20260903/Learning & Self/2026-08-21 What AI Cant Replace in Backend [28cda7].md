# What AI Can't Replace in Backend

> Первый миллион пользователей не ломает код. Он ломает всё вокруг кода.
> — nfactorial.school

**Main idea:** AI can write features in minutes but cannot replace the backend engineering depth needed to debug double-charged payments, choose database indexes, or respond to a 4 AM outage.

## Key points
- Scale breaks everything around the code, not the code itself — process, data integrity, ops
- AI cannot explain why a payment was charged twice or diagnose a live database problem
- Real backend expertise means knowing why a database needs an index and what to do when a service goes down at 4 AM

**Why save:** Sharp framing of the gap between AI-generated code and the production-level backend judgment that keeps systems alive at scale.
