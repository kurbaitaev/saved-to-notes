# Warren Buffett's 5/25 Focus Rule

> if you try to catch two rabbits at once you'll eventually lose both
> — hangxw

**Main idea:** Use Warren Buffett's 5/25 rule to cut goal overwhelm: your top 5 get full attention, everything else goes on an "avoid at all costs" list until you find success there.

## Key points
- Chasing too many goals simultaneously leads to exhaustion and loss of joy in your work
- Unfinished pursuits are not wasted — but they go nowhere without real focus
- The avoid-at-all-costs list is temporary, not permanent — revisit once you've made progress on your top 5

## Steps
1. Write down 25 things you want to do most
2. Circle your top 5
3. Everything not circled becomes your avoid-at-all-costs list until you find success in your top 5
